THE PLUTONIANS — FINAL MODE DESIGN
==================================

GOAL
- Thaw all six established playfield areas at the same time.
- The six areas reuse Super Swami's bottom/middle/upper x left/right map.
- Rooftop access starts open and stays open for the mode.

AREA CONTROL
- All six areas begin frozen.
- One qualifying switch hit thaws an entire frozen area at any time.
- Hits in already-thawed areas receive only normal switch scoring.
- A thaw scores 100,000, or 150,000 with Bigger Jackpots.
- Frozen area indicators are blue; thawed area indicators are yellow.

FREEZE RAY
- While active, the freeze ray fires every 12 seconds.
- Each firing randomly refreezes one currently thawed area.
- If no area is thawed, the firing has no effect.
- Every upper-target hit scores 50,000, or 75,000 with Bigger Jackpots.
- An upper-target hit blocks the ray for 20 seconds, or 25 with More Time.
- Another target hit during the blocked state resets the full blocked duration.
- When blocking expires, a fresh 12-second active firing cycle begins.
- Upper targets pulse red while active and yellow while blocked.

CASE FILES
- More Jackpots: the first upper target hit during each upper-playfield entrance
  awards a 500,000 Space Warp Jackpot (750,000 with Bigger Jackpots).
- Bigger Jackpots: thaw 150,000; upper target 75,000; completion 1,500,000;
  More Jackpots collect 750,000.
- More Time: block duration increases from 20 to 25 seconds.
- Safety Net: 10-second ball save at mode start.
- Shot Assist: the first successful thaw also thaws one random frozen area;
  both receive the full thaw score. One use per mode.

COMPLETION
- The instant all six areas are thawed, stop the freeze-ray timer permanently.
- Award 1,000,000 for repairing the Space Warp Control, or 1,500,000 with Bigger.
- Show SPACE WARP CONTROL REPAIRED.
- Hold the successful mode for two seconds with only normal switch scoring.
- Then end the mode and show the summary.

DISPLAY
- Active instruction: HIT TARGETS TO BLOCK RAY
- Active timer: FREEZE IN X
- Blocked instruction: HIT AREAS TO THAW
- Blocked timer: FREEZE BLOCKED X
- Messages: AREA THAWED, AREA REFROZEN, FREEZE RAY BLOCKED,
  SPACE WARP JACKPOT, SPACE WARP CONTROL REPAIRED.
- Do not show a separate PLUTONIANS DEFEATED victory message.

SUMMARY
- MODE SCORE
- AREAS THAWED
- FREEZE RAY BLOCKERS
