CONNER'S REPTILES — SWAMP RAMPAGE
Final rules sync: August 14, 2026

Core rules

- The rooftop gate remains closed during the Jackpot phase.
- Every physical pop hit scores 25,000, adds 50,000 to the persistent Swamp Bonus,
  and reveals one random unique Jackpot objective that has not already been revealed.
- Pop hits continue scoring and adding to Swamp Bonus throughout the entire mode,
  including after every Jackpot has been revealed and while the VUK Super is lit.
- Revealed Jackpot objectives pulse until collected and remain lit indefinitely.

Base Jackpot objectives

1. Upper A rollover
2. Upper B rollover
3. Middle A rollover
4. Middle B rollover
5. Left web target
6. Center web target
7. Saucers — any of Saucers 1, 2, or 3 collects this single grouped objective

Scoring and completion

- Each revealed Jackpot scores 100,000.
- Collecting all required Jackpot objectives opens the rooftop gate using
  rooftop_diverter_open and lights the VUK for an untimed 500,000 Super.
- Only collecting the VUK Super defeats Conner's Reptiles and sets the mode state
  to completed. Starting the mode or reaching the Super does not mark completion.
- The Super is held in the VUK until the villain summary is finished.

Persistent Swamp Bonus

- Every counted pop hit adds 50,000 to swamp_bonus.
- Swamp Bonus persists across balls and is awarded on the end-of-ball Mode Bonus page.
- Immediate pop scoring and the persistent Swamp Bonus are separate awards.

Case Files

- More Jackpots: adds the star rollover as an eighth required Jackpot objective.
- Bigger Jackpots: each revealed Jackpot scores 150,000 instead of 100,000.
  Pop scoring, Swamp Bonus contributions, and the 500,000 VUK Super do not change.
- More Time: when Safety Net is active, extends its opening ball save from 10 to
  20 seconds.
- Safety Net: 10-second opening ball save.
- Shot Assist: the first physical pop hit counts as two pop hits. It scores 50,000,
  adds 100,000 to Swamp Bonus, and reveals two unique Jackpot objectives.

Lighting

- Revealed rollovers pulse yellow, web targets pulse white, and the grouped saucers
  and optional star pulse red to match their physical lenses.
- Collected objectives turn off.
- When the Super is ready, the Jackpot objective lights clear and the VUK path GI
  flashes green.
