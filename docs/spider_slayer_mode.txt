SPIDER-SLAYER — HUNT MODE

Build phase
- One random shot category starts lit.
- Successful lit shots score 50,000 and add another lit category; earlier lit shots remain active.
- Bigger Jackpots raises successful lit shots to 75,000.
- Hunt Time begins on the first successful lit shot and stops on the fifteenth.
- Shot Assist makes the first successful hit add two new lit categories instead of one.
- Categories: left web, center web, left sling, right sling, left pop, right pop, left bank, right bank, star, and any saucer.
- Any target or rubber in a bank counts for its bank category. Bank resets remain normal.

Slayer Jackpot
- The fifteenth successful shot clears all hunt lights, opens the rooftop gate, and lights the Daily Bugle VUK.
- Base Slayer Jackpot is 2,000,000 and falls by 100,000 per second to a 100,000 minimum.
- Bigger Jackpots starts it at 2,500,000.
- More Time reduces the decay to 50,000 per second.
- More Jackpots freezes the full starting value for ten seconds before decay begins.
- Safety Net starts a ten-second, one-ball save when the VUK lights.
- The display shows the falling jackpot value only; there is no separate timer.
- Collecting the VUK destroys the Spider-Slayer and ends the mode.

Lighting
- Dark red GI during the hunt.
- Active shots pulse red. Bank categories use bank GI lights.
- Successful shots flash the playfield red.
- At fifteen hits, build lights turn off, GI changes green, and the shared
  Master Vine route chase runs from the lower spinner through the ramp to the
  Daily Bugle VUK. It stops on the VUK hit or mode cleanup.

Summary
- MODE SCORE
- SLAYER JP
- HUNT TIME (first successful shot through fifteenth successful shot)
