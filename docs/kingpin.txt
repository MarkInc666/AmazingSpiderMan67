KINGPIN: CLEAR THE CITY

A+B Daily Bugle mystery mode disabled during wizrd mode

Start 2-ball multiball with 20s ball save.

One random city area is active at a time:
- Pops: 10 hits
- Spinner: 10 spins
- Star: 3 hits
- Upper Targets: 2 hits each
- Drops: complete both banks (message: COMPLETE THE DROP TARGET BANKS)

Active area hits score 50K.
Inactive area hits score 20K.

Completing the active area lights Daily Bugle Jackpot at the VUK, opens the gate.
Jackpot = 100K × (balls in play + cleared areas)

Complete A+B before a Daily Bugle/VUK visit to qualify add-a-ball.
If fewer than 5 balls are in play when the VUK is reached, add-a-ball is awarded.
This applies to normal Jackpot play and Victory Laps.
Max 5 balls in play.
10 second ball save when ball added.
A+B is consumed/reset by the VUK visit. A+B lighting is yellow, never red.

Saucers score 50K.
If more than 1 ball is active, saucers hold the ball for 10s, then eject.
If only 1 ball remains, saucers eject immediately.

Upper gate opens only when:
- Daily Bugle Jackpot is ready
- Spinner area is active
- Upper Target area is active (gate must open immediately for this phase)

After all city areas are cleared:
Victory Laps begin.
All areas are lit for 50K per hit.
Status shows Victory Laps / Complete A+B rather than a zero jackpot counter.
A+B opens gate and Daily Bugle Super Jackpot is lit for 1M × balls in play.
A+B may also award add-a-ball at the VUK whenever fewer than 5 balls are in play.

Mode ends when only 1 ball remains.
If a ball is held in a saucer when the mode ends, eject it.
