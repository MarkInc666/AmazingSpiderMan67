SINISTER SURGE
Chapter 1 Mini-Wizard

STATUS
- Coded from the approved callback-stage design.
- Needs on-machine playtest and timing/lighting verification.

START / END
- Starts 2-ball multiball.
- Initial ball save: 20 seconds.
- Mode ends when multiball ends (one ball remains).
- Daily Bugle mystery is disabled while Sinister Surge owns the VUK.
- Setup kicks a ball already sitting at the VUK.

STAGE FLOW
- One random unfinished Chapter 1 villain stage is active at a time.
- Completing a stage lights the Daily Bugle/VUK Jackpot.
- Collect the Jackpot before another unfinished stage is chosen.

RHINO
- 6 pop hits light Jackpot.

SANDMAN
- Uses the right 5-bank.
- One special target flashes.
- Every 4 seconds the special target advances to the next standing drop.
- Hit the currently flashing target to light Jackpot.
- If all five targets are down without collecting the lit target, reset the bank
  and continue the Sandman attempt.

VULTURE
- Hit any one upper target once.
- Make 3 upper-spinner spins.
- Target and spinner requirements may be completed in either order.
- Completing both requirements lights Jackpot.

ELECTRO
- Left web and center web are the two shots.
- One starts lit.
- Hit the lit web; the other web becomes lit and a 20-second timer starts.
- Hit the second lit web before timeout to light Jackpot.
- Timeout resets the Electro attempt and allows an immediate restart.

GREEN GOBLIN
- Any saucer starts the attempt and holds that ball for 4 seconds.
- Four areas flash green: left web, center web, left drop bank, right drop bank.
- Any individual drop hit qualifies its bank; each of the four areas counts once.
- Hit 2 of the 4 areas before the 4-second hold ends to light Jackpot.
- Success releases the held ball immediately.
- Failure releases the held ball at 4 seconds and allows another saucer restart.

SAUCER REST
- During any non-Goblin stage, one ball may rest in any saucer for up to 20 seconds.
- Only one saucer ball may be resting at a time.
- A ball entering another saucer while one is already held ejects normally.
- The rest does not pause the current stage or its timers.
- Green Goblin replaces the rest with its specific 4-second attempt hold.

JACKPOTS / ADD-A-BALL
- Stage Jackpot = 100K x (balls in play + stages cleared) + Case File bonus.
- Complete A+B before collecting a stage Jackpot to add a ball on that Jackpot.
- Maximum balls in play: 5.
- Added-ball save: 10 seconds.
- A+B resets after each stage Jackpot.

VICTORY LAPS
- Begin after all five villain stages have been completed and their Jackpots collected.
- A+B lights the Daily Bugle/VUK Super Jackpot.
- Super Jackpot = 1M x balls in play + Case File bonus.

CASE FILE VALUE BONUS
- Each accumulated chapter Case File contributes 20K to every stage Jackpot and
  Super Jackpot through mini_wizard_case_file_bonus.
- Shared chapter cap: 25 Case Files / +500K.

SCORING
- Active qualifying stage hits: 50K.
- Other registered stage-related hits: 20K.
- Saucer entry: 50K.
- Victory Lap registered hits: 50K.

LIGHTING
- Rhino: pop lighting.
- Sandman: only the current special right-bank target flashes; green-lens inserts
  use green/off.
- Vulture: upper-target and upper-spinner indicators flash independently and stop
  when their requirement is completed.
- Electro: only the currently required web flashes.
- Goblin: saucers flash while ready; during a 4-second attempt, the four qualifying
  areas flash green and each collected area turns off.
- Saucer main inserts use green and the red hole inserts use red, matching the
  physical lenses in config/lights.yaml.
