DOCTOR COOL MODE
================

Implementation status
---------------------
Implemented for MPF 0.80. Physical machine testing remains required for the
moving saucer window and device timing.

Core rules and scoring
----------------------
- The Frozen Diamond Jackpot starts at 100K, or 200K with Bigger Jackpots.
- Every drop scores 25K and banks 25K to the persistent Diamond Bonus.
- Right drops add 25K to the Jackpot, or 50K with Bigger Jackpots.
- Left drops add 40K to the Jackpot, or 75K with Bigger Jackpots.
- Any right drop starts the moving frozen-shipment chase.
- A wrong saucer scores 10K, ejects, and resumes the chase after one second.
- A correct saucer collects the current Frozen Diamond Jackpot.
- Three shipments complete the normal mode. More Jackpots requires four shipments.
- A terminal saucer is held until the villain summary finishes.

Saucer availability progression
-------------------------------
- The chase begins with one moving available saucer.
- Completing and resetting the right 5-bank arms the next availability upgrade.
- The first right drop after that reset expands the moving window to two saucers.
- Completing/resetting the bank again arms the next upgrade.
- The first following right drop expands the moving window to three saucers.
- Availability is capped at three.
- With one available saucer, the window cycles 1, 2, 3.
- With two, it cycles 1+2, 2+3, 3+1.
- With three, all three saucers remain valid.

Saucer lighting
---------------
Only the two red hole lamps at each available saucer pulse red. The green
number inserts l_1, l_2, and l_3 stay off. Wrong-saucer and shipment-collected
feedback also use only the red hole lamps.

STAR freeze
-----------
STAR always banks 100K to Diamond Bonus. During a chase it freezes the current
one-, two-, or three-saucer availability window for 10 seconds, or 15 seconds
with More Time. STAR does not make unavailable saucers valid.

Case Files
----------
- More Jackpots: requires a fourth frozen shipment.
- Bigger Jackpots: 200K base; +50K right drop; +75K left drop.
- More Time: extends STAR freeze to 15 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts the shared 10-second ball save.
- Shot Assist: the first wrong saucer collects the shipment.

Preserved behavior
------------------
The patch preserves the existing scoring, shipment count, chase speeds, one-
second wrong-saucer resume, Diamond Bonus banking, red STAR flash, VUK feed,
Daily Bugle Mystery ownership, completion delay cleanup, and terminal summary
hold.
