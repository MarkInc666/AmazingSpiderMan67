🎭 MYSTERIO — ILLUSION TRIAL

Mode flow
- Seven shot groups are lit: left web, left pop, right pop,
  left drops, right drops, saucers, and upper targets.
- One random shot is the hidden Mysterio Super Jackpot.
- Five random non-Super shots provide the existing directional Spidey-Sense clues.
- One random non-Super shot provides no useful clue and calls out “He must be here somewhere.”
- The first attempted shot can never collect the Super. If it was the initially
  selected location, the hidden Super moves to another active shot before the
  attempt is resolved.
- Neither the main spinner nor the upper spinner is a Mysterio shot. Left and
  right pops are separate shot groups.

Scoring
- Super starts at 1,000,000 and cannot fall below 100,000.
- Every incorrect shot scores 50,000 and lowers the Super by 100,000.
- Clue shots use the same score and penalty as other incorrect shots, then provide their directional clue.
- Every incorrect shot counts as a Clue Shot in the villain summary, including
  the one non-directional “He must be here somewhere” location.
- Collecting the hidden shot awards the current Super value and completes Mysterio.

Case Files
- More Jackpots: the first incorrect shot still scores 50,000 but does not reduce the Super.
- Bigger Jackpots: Super starts at 1,500,000.
- More Time: incorrect-shot penalties are reduced to 50,000.
- Safety Net: starts a one-time 10-second ball save with the mode.
- Shot Assist: removes the one false, non-clue shot at mode start.

Presentation and completion
- Keep the existing Mysterio shot-lighting style.
- Status shows remaining active shots and the current Super value.
- Correct shot displays “MYSTERIO SUPER JACKPOT” and the collected value.
- The ball remains live while Mysterio rules stop scoring for a 2-second completion hold, then the normal villain-complete summary begins.
- Ball-ending teardown skips the completion delay and stops immediately.
- Ordinary, incorrect, and disabled saucer hits use delayed clearing. If the
  saucers are the hidden Super, the ball remains held through the villain
  summary and is kicked out when that summary finishes.

## Lighting clarification

- Mysterio has seven possible mystery locations; center web is not one of them.
- Saucer mystery lighting uses only the six saucer hole lamps. They flash white; the 1/2/3 number inserts are not used.
- Directional clues are LEFT, RIGHT, or UPPER only.
