The Fifth Avenue Phantom — Vanish and Reveal
============================================

Implementation status
---------------------
Implemented in MPF 0.80-compatible Python/YAML. Needs machine playtest for
shot-travel timing, switch behavior, lamp readability, and overall pacing.

Approved gameplay
-----------------
- Five rounds are played; More Jackpots adds a sixth.
- Each round secretly selects left web, center web, left drops, upper targets,
  or all three saucers as the Phantom location.
- The first unique right drop reveals that location for 8 seconds and sets the
  normal Jackpot to 1M. More Time makes the first reveal 10 seconds.
- Later unique right drops add 8, 10, 12, and 14 seconds respectively while
  lowering the normal Jackpot to 800K, 600K, 400K, and 200K.
- More Time still adds two seconds to every right-drop reveal/add, so later
  additions become 10, 12, 14, and 16 seconds.
- STAR is never a hiding location. It gives a repeatable live 4-second reveal
  or adds 4 seconds to an active reveal without lowering the Jackpot.
- Collecting the revealed location awards the current Jackpot and advances.
- Expiry misses the current round and advances. The final collect or miss ends
  the mode, giving the mode a clear five/six-round ending.

Case Files
----------
- More Jackpots: adds round six.
- Bigger Jackpots: uses 1M, 900K, 800K, 700K, and 600K reveal values.
- More Time: adds two seconds to every right-drop reveal/add.
- Safety Net: starts the shared 10-second Case File ball save.
- Shot Assist: awards the first revealed Jackpot that expires.

Lighting
--------
- Right-bank GI pulses continuously.
- Standing right-drop inserts flash green; collected drop inserts stay green.
- The live Phantom location flashes purple through white lenses.
- SAUCERS flashes only the six red saucer-hole lamps, not green number inserts.
- STAR flashes red throughout the mode to indicate its repeatable reveal role.

Validation boundary
-------------------
Static Python, YAML, duplicate-key, event-coverage, workbook, and ZIP checks
are part of the patch workflow. Machine testing is still required.
