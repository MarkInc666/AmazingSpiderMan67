THE FANTASTIC FAKIR — RUBY HEIST

Core rules
- Starts 2-ball multiball with a 10-second opening ball save.
- The three saucers are fake rubies. Hitting one scores 25,000, holds that ball,
  opens the rooftop gate, and reveals the matching real Ruby target:
  Saucer 1 -> upper-left; Saucer 2 -> upper-center; Saucer 3 -> upper-right.
- The shared VUK route chase starts with the gate opening and guides the live
  ball from the lower spinner route toward the VUK/rooftop entrance.
- The chase stops when the live ball enters the rooftop, when the Ruby attempt
  resolves or expires, when multiball ends, and during mode cleanup.
- The Ruby countdown starts only when the live ball enters the rooftop. It lasts
  10 seconds, or 15 seconds with More Time.
- Either spinner adds 25,000 to the active Ruby or Super Jackpot.
- A wrong upper target does not end the attempt; the correct target remains lit.
- A successful collect holds the saucer for 2 seconds before releasing it.
- If the other live ball enters a different saucer while one ball is held, that
  second saucer ejects after 350ms without starting another Ruby attempt.

Jackpot progression
- Ruby Jackpot base: 250,000, or 400,000 with Bigger Jackpots.
- Collect three Ruby Jackpots to qualify one Super Ruby Jackpot.
- The Super starts at the sum of the first three collected Ruby values, including
  their spinner builds. Additional spinner hits continue building the Super.
- After the Super is collected, ordinary base-value Rubies continue until
  multiball ends. No additional Supers are qualified.
- The mode ends when Fakir multiball returns to one ball.

Case Files
- More Jackpots: the first post-Super Ruby scores 2X.
- Bigger Jackpots: Ruby base becomes 400,000.
- More Time: rooftop Ruby countdown becomes 15 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts one ball save after the first held-saucer release.
- Shot Assist: the first reveal lights all three upper targets as valid.

Lighting
- Base GI is DDDDDD.
- Available saucer hole lamps pulse red; the locked saucer uses its red hole
  lamps plus its white number insert.
- The revealed upper target lights red. Ruby-search GI rapidly sweeps white to
  red and back; a successful Jackpot flashes the GI white three times.
- The VUK route uses the shared green final-VUK chase only while rooftop access
  is being sought.
