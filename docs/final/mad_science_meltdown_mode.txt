MAD SCIENCE MELTDOWN - CHAPTER 7 WIZARD
========================================

Status
------
Substantive implementation. The mode is a repeating scoring multiball and has
no final objective. It completes when the main multiball ends.

Start and ending
----------------
- Starts a 3-ball multiball.
- Opening ball save: 20 seconds.
- Add-a-Balls receive a 10-second save.
- The mode supports up to 4 balls in play.
- The mode ends when multiball ends (fewer than 2 balls remain in play).
- Mode score and Jackpot counts feed the standard chapter-wizard summary.

Chapter Case File bonus
-----------------------
- No normal Case File helper effects are used in this wizard.
- Every Chapter 7 Case File adds 20,000 to every Gas and Noah Jackpot.
- The shared mini_wizard_case_file_bonus player value supplies this amount.

Gas system - Doctor Dumpty and Professor Pretorius
---------------------------------------------------
Gas zones:
- Left web
- Center web
- Left pop
- Right pop
- Left sling
- Right sling

Each newly knocked-down left-bank target releases gas into one randomly chosen
inactive zone. The new zone begins red. Gas zones progress independently, so
red, yellow and green zones may exist at the same time.

Every lower-spinner spin cools every active zone by one step:
- Red -> yellow
- Yellow -> green
- Green remains green until collected

Gas scoring:
- Hit a red zone: 25,000; it remains red.
- Hit a yellow zone: 50,000; it remains yellow.
- Hit a green zone: Gas Jackpot of 500,000 plus 20,000 per Chapter Case File;
  the zone is cleared.

Completing the left 3-bank closes the rooftop gate and immediately resets the
bank. The reset bank can begin releasing additional gas. When all active gas
zones have been collected, the rooftop gate reopens unless Noah is revealed.
If all six gas zones are active, another left-drop hit cannot release a seventh.

Noah Boddy search and Jackpot
-----------------------------
- The right 5-bank starts standing.
- Noah is not preselected. The last standing target becomes Noah.
- Each upper-target hit physically drops one random standing false location.
- A direct lower-playfield hit on a standing right target also eliminates it as
  a false location.
- When only one target remains standing, Noah is revealed and the gate closes.
- Hit the final standing right target to collect the Noah Boddy Jackpot.
- The right bank resets and the search repeats.

Noah Jackpot before containment multiplication:
  500,000 + Chapter Case File bonus + 100,000 per upper-spinner spin

The upper-spinner build resets after each Noah Jackpot. Jackpot-build messages
use the ordinary mode-message presentation; only the collected Jackpot uses
the Jackpot presentation and audio.

Saucer containment - Dr. Von Schlick
------------------------------------
- Any saucer may park its entering ball while at least one other ball remains
  loose and playable.
- No more than two balls may be parked.
- Noah multiplier by parked-ball count:
  - 0 parked: 1X
  - 1 parked: 2X
  - 2 parked: 3X
- The Case File bonus and upper-spinner build are included before multiplication.
- Collecting Noah releases all parked balls.
- A repeating deadlock guard releases a parked ball if no loose ball remains.
- All saucers are released when the mode stops.

Magneto Add-a-Ball
------------------
- Complete Dr. Magneto's middle A and B rollovers.
- Center web lights for 10 seconds.
- Center web with fewer than 4 balls in play: Add-a-Ball with a 10-second save.
- Center web with 4 balls already in play: award 1,000,000 instead.
- Parked balls count toward the 4-ball limit.
- After collection or timeout, A and B reset and may be qualified again.
- If center web is also an active gas zone, the same switch processes both the
  gas award and the Magneto award.

Gate and VUK safety
-------------------
- The gate starts open.
- Left-bank completion closes it.
- Clearing all active gas can reopen it.
- Revealing Noah closes it.
- Collecting Noah reopens it only when no gas remains active.
- Gate movement uses rooftop_diverter_open / rooftop_diverter_close only.
- A VUK ball is ejected through the shared request_vuk_eject path.

Lighting
--------
- Left-bank GI pulses red to identify the gas-release bank.
- Lower spinner and its GI pulse white/blue for cooling.
- Active gas areas pulse red, yellow or green; green pulses faster to indicate
  a collectible Jackpot.
- Standing right-bank inserts pulse dim green. The final Noah target pulses
  bright green at double speed.
- Upper-target inserts pulse amber while false locations remain.
- The open-gate VUK route uses a visible blue chase.
- Middle A and B use their yellow-lens inserts. Center web uses the shared white
  approach chase during its 10-second award window.
- Parked saucers light their matching green 1/2/3 inserts.

Summary variables
-----------------
- active_mode_points: total wizard score
- active_mode_hits: Gas Jackpots plus Noah Jackpots
- active_mode_major_hits: Noah Jackpots
- mad_science_meltdown_gas_jackpots
- mad_science_meltdown_noah_jackpots
- mad_science_meltdown_active_gas_zones
- mad_science_meltdown_gas_red / gas_yellow / gas_green
- mad_science_meltdown_parked_balls
- mad_science_meltdown_jackpot_value
- mad_science_meltdown_magneto_seconds
