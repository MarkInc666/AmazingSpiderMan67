DR. MAGNETO (MODE 32)
=====================

GAMEPLAY
- Left sling or left inlane lights right-side A; right sling or right inlane lights left-side B.
- Either A rollover (upper A or middle-right A) collects the lit A objective; either B rollover (upper B or middle-left B) collects the lit B objective.
- Both A inserts light together, as do both B inserts. A feeds the left pop and B feeds the right pop.
- A/B and flashing-pop objectives last 12 seconds, or 15 seconds with More Time.
- A missed rollover returns to the sling/inlane qualifier. A missed pop relights its rollover for another attempt; completed pops stay completed.
- Rollover awards are 100K, or 150K with Bigger Jackpots. Each completed pop awards 250K.
- Completing both pops lights the center-web Super for 24 seconds, or 30 seconds with More Time.
- The Super is 500K, or 750K with Bigger Jackpots. Collection scores and plays immediately, then Dr. Magneto completes after a 2-second final-award hold; expiry ends the mode.

CASE FILES
- More Jackpots: each newly lit objective also lights STAR for six seconds at 250K.
- Bigger Jackpots: 150K rollovers and 750K Super.
- More Time: 15-second circuit objectives and 30-second Super.
- Safety Net: 10-second opening ball save.
- Shot Assist: the first qualifier hit lights both A and B.

LIGHTING AND STATUS
- Overall mode GI is solid purple 6030A0.
- The available left and right sling/inlane qualifier areas pulse bright purple over the base GI.
- A/B and flashing-pop objectives begin at normal pulse speed and double after six seconds.
- Completed pop rings remain solid blue.
- The center-web Super uses a white four-light chase: multibonus, advance multiplier, special, center web.
- The chase begins at 250ms per step and doubles in speed after twelve seconds.
- More Jackpots STAR remains a fast red pulse for its six-second window.
- The persistent Super status updates every second with the live countdown.

VALIDATION STATUS
- Static validation is required after patching.
- Physical machine testing remains required for cue readability and timing.
