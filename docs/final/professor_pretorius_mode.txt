PROFESSOR PRETORIUS — REACTOR EXPERIMENT

STATUS
Implemented. Physical-machine playtest still required.

CORE FLOW
- Complete the left pop, right pop, left bank, and right bank reactor stations.
- Each reactor hit raises temperature by 1; spinner hits cool it by 1.
- GI communicates temperature from green at TEMP 0 through yellow/orange to red
  at TEMP 5, while also pulsing faster as the reactor gets hotter.
- At TEMP 5, an overheat grace starts: 4 seconds normally or 8 seconds with
  More Time. The persistent status counts down the remaining grace time.
- Completing all four stations opens rooftop access and lights the VUK for the
  500,000 Reactor Super.
- Reactor Super is timed: 15 seconds normally or 20 seconds with More Time.
  Its remaining time is shown in the persistent mode status. Expiry fails the
  mode if the VUK Super is not collected.
- The shared Master Vine route chase runs from the lower spinner through the
  ramp to the VUK while the Reactor Super is ready.
- Temperature/overheat remains active during the Super phase; if both timers
  are active, the status shows both the Super time and overheat time.
- The chase stops immediately on the VUK hit, failure, or mode cleanup.

CASE FILES
- More Jackpots: all five right-bank targets score reactor hits.
- Bigger Jackpots: reactor hits score 150,000.
- More Time: extends overheat grace from 4 to 8 seconds and Reactor Super from
  15 to 20 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts a 10-second opening ball save.
- Shot Assist: the first left drop adds one random remaining left drop.
