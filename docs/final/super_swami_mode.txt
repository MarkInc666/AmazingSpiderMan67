SUPER SWAMI — RESTORE NEW YORK

STATUS
Implemented. Physical-machine playtest still required.

CORE FLOW
- Restore all six playfield areas before the city timer expires.
- Every unrestored area's dim `404040` GI independently flickers to full white
  for 100ms at random intervals between 2 and 5 seconds. Restoring an area
  immediately stops its flicker and leaves that region solid white.
- A persistent `CITY TIME` status shows the live 40/50-second countdown and the
  number of restored regions. During the Blackout finale it changes its objective
  to `BLACKOUT AT DAILY BUGLE` while continuing the same countdown.
- Without More Jackpots, restoring the sixth area completes the mode normally.
- With More Jackpots, restoring the sixth area opens rooftop access and lights
  the Daily Bugle VUK for the 500,000 Blackout Jackpot.
- Rooftop access uses one guarded `rooftop_diverter_open` event; the obsolete
  500ms and 1,500ms gate retries are not used.
- The shared Master Vine route chase runs from the lower spinner through the
  ramp to the VUK while the Blackout Jackpot is lit.
- The chase stops immediately on the VUK hit, timer failure, or mode cleanup.

CASE FILES
- More Jackpots: adds the final 500,000 Blackout Jackpot at the VUK.
- Bigger Jackpots: restoration values begin at 200,000.
- More Time: extends the city timer from 40 to 50 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts the opening ball save.
- Shot Assist: the first restored area also spots another unlit area.
