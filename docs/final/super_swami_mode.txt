SUPER SWAMI — RESTORE NEW YORK

STATUS
Implemented; physical-machine playtesting remains unconfirmed.

CORE FLOW
- Restore all six playfield areas before the city timer expires (40 seconds, or 50 with More Time).
- Blackout regional GI is solid 707070; unrestored regions flicker briefly to FFFFFF for 200ms every 2–5 seconds.
- Restored regions become solid F0F0F0, with flickering stopped for that area.
- Each restored area awards 150K, 225K, 300K, 375K, 450K, then 525K (2.025M total).
- Bigger Jackpots adds 100K to each award: 250K, 325K, 400K, 475K, 550K, 625K (2.625M total).
- Shot Assist automatically restores one additional area after the first successful restoration; the assisted area awards its full normal or Bigger Jackpots score, updates progress and appears in the mode summary.
- A persistent CITY TIME status shows the countdown and restored-area progress.
- Without More Jackpots, the sixth restoration completes the mode immediately.
- With More Jackpots, the sixth restoration opens the rooftop, lights the Daily Bugle VUK and starts a separate 15-second Blackout Jackpot collection timer (regardless of time left on the city timer).
- The Blackout Jackpot is 750K, or 1M with Bigger Jackpots. Failure to collect it in 15 seconds ends the mode.
- The Blackout VUK route chase uses 707070 for inactive route lights and FFFFFF for the moving highlight; it stops on VUK collection, timer failure or mode cleanup.
- A VUK hit that qualifies upper-left restoration cannot also collect the newly lit Blackout Jackpot on the same switch event.

CASE FILES
- More Jackpots: separate 15-second Blackout Jackpot at VUK after all six areas are restored.
- Bigger Jackpots: increases restoration values by 100K each and the Blackout Jackpot to 1M.
- More Time: extends city timer from 40 to 50 seconds (does not alter the 15-second finale).
- Safety Net: 30-second opening ball save.
- Shot Assist: first restoration also spots and fully scores one additional area.
