BRUTUS — GET PAST THE BODYGUARD
Implemented: August 13, 2026

MODE IDENTITY

Brutus guards stolen artwork in all three saucers. The player must lure him
away with the right drop bank, recover one artwork before he returns, and avoid
the left drop bank while the saucers are open.

CORE LOOP

1. Begin with all five right drops standing and all saucers guarded.
2. Hit any right drop for 50K. The other four targets drop programmatically.
3. All three saucers light for 15 seconds.
4. Shoot any saucer for a 250K Artwork Jackpot.
5. Close the window, reset the right bank, and repeat.

Three Artwork Jackpots defeat Brutus. The mode changes state before firing the
four automatic knockdown coils, so their switch closures cannot create extra
lures or additional windows.

BRUTUS RETURNS

- Any left drop hit during an open window immediately turns off the saucers and
  resets the right bank without awarding the Artwork Jackpot.
- If the 15-second window expires, the same round restarts with the right bank
  reset. Collected Artwork Jackpots are preserved.
- Left drops do not affect Brutus while the saucers are guarded.
- A guarded saucer scores 25K, displays BLOCKED BY BRUTUS, and ejects quickly.

CASE FILES

- More Jackpots: require four Artwork Jackpots instead of three.
- Bigger Jackpots: Artwork Jackpots score 400K instead of 250K.
- More Time: open saucer windows last 20 seconds instead of 15.
- Safety Net: start the standard 10-second Case File ball save.
- Shot Assist: two seconds after mode start, programmatically drop all five
  right-bank targets and open the first saucer window. It applies only once.

DEVICE AND PRESENTATION RULES

- The rooftop gate remains closed throughout Brutus.
- The VUK is not a mode shot and ejects after a neutral one-second delay.
- Green right-bank inserts pulse while Brutus is guarding the artwork.
- During an open window, the red-lensed hole lamps and green saucer-number
  inserts flash; the left-bank GI flashes as the danger shot.
- The mode uses the Brutus portrait rather than inherited Lizard footage.

SCORING SUMMARY

Right-drop lure:                    50K
Guarded saucer:                     25K
Artwork Jackpot:                   250K
Artwork Jackpot with Bigger:       400K
