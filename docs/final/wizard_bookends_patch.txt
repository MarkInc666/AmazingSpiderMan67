INSTALL

project, merging folders and replacing the matching files. Keep the three new

PRESENTATION
All 11 chapter wizards and Final Showdown now use wizard_intro.tscn.
Wizard intros: 6 seconds; existing double-flipper skip and instruction recall
still work. Gameplay starts with each wizard's existing assigned song.

Chapter wizard summaries: 7 seconds in wizard_summary.tscn, followed by the
existing 3-second Comic COLLECTED screen. Total: 10 seconds. Summary music runs
across both screens; it does not restart at the comic transition. Existing
physical-ball cleanup still occurs at the transition to Comic COLLECTED, and
progression/done events still wait until the comic screen has finished.

The summary echoes the chapter panel's navy rows, red header, yellow heading,
and green checkmarks. All five names are shown together. Names/order come from
the live villain_progression.CHAPTERS and VILLAINS definitions, matched by
wizard key, rather than the selected chapter or a duplicated list. Snapshot
machine variables prevent chapter transitions or test-harness starts from
showing stale player-panel values. Values use thousands separators.

Final Showdown uses wizard_final_summary.tscn, without a chapter roster or
Comic COLLECTED screen. Its existing 6-second summary timing is retained.
Ordinary villain intros, summaries, songs, and timings remain as before.
