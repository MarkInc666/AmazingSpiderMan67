PARDO - HYPNOSIS REEL
=====================

OBJECTIVE
---------
- Play four Hypnosis rounds. More Jackpots adds two extra recovery rounds.
- Each round presents three lit shots selected from seven logical shot groups.
- One of the three shots is secretly correct.
- The spinner reveals which shot is correct.
- Four correct rounds defeats Pardo immediately.
- If all available rounds expire before four correct rounds, Pardo escapes.

ROUND FLOW
----------
- All three available choices flash green identically.
- Hitting the correct shot immediately collects the current Hypnosis Jackpot and ends the round.
- The first incorrect shot scores 20K, turns that choice off, and reduces the current Jackpot.
- The round then continues with the two remaining choices.
- The spinner can reveal the correct choice again while two shots remain.
- Hitting the correct shot now collects the reduced Jackpot and ends the round.
- Hitting the remaining incorrect shot scores 20K and ends the round as a failure.

REVEAL
------
- A spinner hit reveals the correct shot for 4 seconds.
- More Time extends the reveal to 6 seconds.
- Hidden choices flash green together so all currently available target shots are clearly selectable while preserving a consistent visible color through the white and green physical lenses.
- During reveal, the correct shot flashes green and the wrong choices turn off.
- After a first wrong choice, the eliminated choice goes dark and only the two surviving choices continue flashing.
- A correct or wrong choice briefly flashes one GI section green or red, then restores Pardo's normal GI color after 350ms.

SCORING
-------
Normal Hypnosis Jackpot values by round:
- Round 1: 200K clean / 100K after one wrong choice
- Round 2: 300K clean / 200K after one wrong choice
- Round 3: 400K clean / 300K after one wrong choice
- Round 4: 500K clean / 400K after one wrong choice
- Extra More-Jackpots rounds retain the Round 4 value.

Bigger Jackpots values by round:
- Round 1: 300K clean / 150K after one wrong choice
- Round 2: 450K clean / 300K after one wrong choice
- Round 3: 600K clean / 450K after one wrong choice
- Round 4: 750K clean / 600K after one wrong choice
- Extra More-Jackpots rounds retain the Round 4 value.

- Every incorrect choice scores 20K.
- The summary reports first-guess Jackpots, second-guess Jackpots, and total mode points.

CASE FILES
----------
- More JP: six available rounds instead of four, giving two recovery rounds to reach four correct rounds.
- Bigger JP: uses the 300K/450K/600K/750K Jackpot ladder; a first wrong reduces the current Jackpot by 150K.
- More Time: the spinner reveal lasts 6 seconds instead of 4 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts the standard 10-second case-file ball save.
- Shot Assist: all three choices are treated as correct during the first round.

SHOTS
-----
- Left web
- Center web
- Upper-target group (left, center, and right upper targets always share one state)
- Left drop bank
- Right drop bank
- Left pop
- Right pop

DEVICE BEHAVIOR
---------------
- Rooftop access opens whenever the upper-target group is one of the current choices.
- If the upper-target choice is removed by a wrong hit, rooftop access closes unless another active rule needs it.
- The Daily Bugle VUK uses the shared regular release delay.
- Saucers are not choices and use the shared saucer kickout action.
