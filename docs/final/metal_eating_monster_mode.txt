METAL-EATING MONSTER — ZONES UNDER ATTACK

Core rules
- Eight playfield zones can be attacked once each.
- The first attack begins after 5 seconds. While fewer than four zones have
  been saved, another safe zone comes under attack every 5 seconds.
- Each attack lasts 12 seconds. Hitting any mapped switch in that zone saves it
  for 250,000; an expired attack permanently destroys the zone.
- A save resets the next-attack delay to 2 seconds.
- Saving four zones stops new attacks. The player wins after every remaining
  active attack has resolved. Three destroyed zones ends the mode immediately.
- Multiple zones may be under attack at the same time.

Zone mapping
1. Left sling: left sling and left inlane.
2. Right sling: right sling, right inlane, and right return.
3. Left web: left web, left B, and the adjacent left area.
4. Upper right: STAR, A/B rollovers, and the upper-right area.
5. Left bank: all left-bank targets/rubbers and the main spinner route.
6. Right bank: all right-bank targets/rubbers and the right-side return area.
7. Center: both pops and the center web.
8. Saucers: all three saucers and their approach area.

Case Files
- Bigger Jackpots: zone saves score 400,000.
- More Time: each zone timer lasts 16 seconds.
- More Jackpots: each saved zone relights once for half its save value.
- Safety Net: starts a 10-second, one-ball save.
- Shot Assist: automatically saves the first attacked zone that reaches
  2 seconds remaining.

Lighting
- Base mode GI is dim white (303030).
- Attacked zones flash every 250ms. Fixed-color inserts use their native lens
  colors and surrounding RGB GI flashes orange at 100% brightness.
- Saved zones keep their native inserts solid. Surrounding RGB GI is solid
  green at 75% brightness (00BF00).
- A More Jackpots repeat opportunity pulses the saved zone at 600ms while
  retaining the same 75% green GI level.
- Destroyed zones are dark.
