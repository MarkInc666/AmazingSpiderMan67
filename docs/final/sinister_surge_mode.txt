SINISTER SURGE
Chapter 1 Mini-Wizard

STATUS
- Coded from the reviewed Chapter 1 callback-stage design.
- Needs on-machine playtest and timing/lighting verification.

START / END
- Starts 2-ball multiball.
- Initial ball save: 20 seconds.
- Mode ends when multiball ends (one ball remains).
- Daily Bugle mystery is disabled while Sinister Surge owns the VUK.
- Setup kicks a ball already sitting at the VUK.
- Maximum balls in play: 4.

STAGE FLOW
- One random unfinished Chapter 1 villain stage is active at a time.
- Except for Rhino's special Berserk/VUK resolution, completing a stage lights the
  Daily Bugle/VUK Jackpot.
- Collect the Jackpot before another unfinished stage is chosen.

RHINO
- Five pop hits build Rage 1-5; every pop advances one level.
- Pop rings pulse faster and become progressively redder as Rage builds.
- A/B inserts build cumulatively like the main Rhino mode:
  Rage 2 upper A, Rage 3 upper B, Rage 4 middle A, Rage 5 middle B.
- At Rage 5 Rhino goes BERSERK for 10 seconds and the rooftop gate opens.
- Safe route: hit any A/B during Berserk to lock 1x Rhino Jackpot and stop the
  Berserk timer; the VUK then remains lit for another 1x Jackpot (2x total).
- Risk route: shoot the VUK directly during Berserk for 3x Rhino Jackpot and
  immediately finish the Rhino stage.
- If Berserk times out, the Rhino stage resets to Rage 0 and must be built again.

SANDMAN
- Uses the right 5-bank.
- One special target flashes.
- Every 4 seconds the special target advances to the next standing drop.
- Hit the currently flashing target to light Jackpot.
- If all five targets are down without collecting the lit target, reset the bank
  and continue the Sandman attempt.
- The upper/lower right-bank GI pulses softly yellow/white throughout the stage.

VULTURE
- Hit any one upper target once.
- Make 3 upper-spinner spins.
- Target and spinner requirements may be completed in either order.
- Completing both requirements lights Jackpot.
- Gate is open during the Vulture stage.

ELECTRO
- A single Spark roams among six lower-playfield shot groups:
  left web, center web, left pop, right pop, left bank, right bank.
- The active Spark stays at each location for about 4 seconds, then moves.
- Hitting the active Spark scores one successful chase hit and immediately moves it.
- Three successful Spark hits complete Electro and light Jackpot.
- Missing the active location does not reset progress; the Spark simply continues roaming.

GREEN GOBLIN
- Entering Goblin releases all previously parked balls, staggered 300ms apart.
- Empty eligible saucers flash white while waiting for a fresh capture.
- The challenge ball is tracked separately from ordinary parked balls.
- Number inserts l_1/l_2/l_3 are not used for the Goblin saucer indication.
- Any saucer starts the attempt and holds that ball for 10 seconds.
- Four areas flash green: left web, center web, left drop bank, right drop bank.
- Any individual drop hit qualifies its bank; each of the four areas counts once.
- Hit 2 of the 4 areas before the 10-second safe-time hold ends to light Jackpot.
- Success releases the held ball immediately.
- Failure releases the held ball at 10 seconds and allows another saucer restart.

SAUCER PARKING
- Up to three balls may park, provided at least one ball remains loose.
- Capacity: 1 ball in play = 0 parked; 2 = 1; 3 = 2; 4 = 3.
- Each ordinary parked ball has its own 20-second timer, including additional
  parking during an active Goblin challenge. Parking does not pause stage timers.
- A 250ms watchdog releases the oldest parked ball if no loose ball remains.
- If that safety release is Goblin's challenge ball, reset the attempt.
- Ordinary parking releases never cancel Goblin's independent 10-second timer.
- Mode end cancels parking/challenge timers and requests staggered releases.
- Shared occupancy-checked requests execute the saucer ejects.

JACKPOTS / ADD-A-BALL
- Standard stage Jackpot = 100K x (balls in play + stages cleared) + Case File bonus.
- Outside Victory Laps, complete A+B to add a ball with 2 or 3 balls in play.
- At the four-ball cap, retain A+B qualification until a drain creates room.
- Add-a-Ball resets A+B and can be earned again below the cap.
- Maximum balls in play: 4.
- Added-ball save: 10 seconds.
- Rhino's direct Berserk VUK awards 3x the Rhino stage Jackpot and resolves the stage
  without creating an additional standard VUK Jackpot afterward.

VICTORY LAPS
- Begin after all five villain stages have been completed and resolved.
- Transition message: ALL VILLAINS DEFEATED!, then after 2 seconds VICTORY LAPS / COMPLETE A + B.
- GI continuously fades white -> red -> white -> blue -> white and repeats.
- Registered playfield hits score 50K.
- Complete A+B to light the Daily Bugle/VUK Super Jackpot and open the gate.
- Super Jackpot = 1M x balls in play + Case File bonus.
- A+B resets after each Super and the cycle repeats.

CASE FILE VALUE BONUS
- Each accumulated chapter Case File contributes 20K to every standard stage Jackpot and
  Super Jackpot through mini_wizard_case_file_bonus.
- Shared chapter cap: 25 Case Files / +500K.

SCORING
- Active qualifying stage hits: 50K.
- Other registered stage-related hits: 20K.
- Saucer entry: 50K.
- Victory Lap registered hits: 50K.

LIGHTING
- Rhino: pop rings become faster/redder as Rage rises; A/Bs build cumulatively and
  reach full white at Berserk.
- Sandman: current right-bank target flashes while right-bank GI pulses yellow/white.
- Vulture: upper-target and upper-spinner indicators flash independently and stop
  when their requirement is completed.
- Electro: only the current roaming Spark location flashes; bank inserts respect
  their physical green lenses while webs/pops use yellow.
- Saucers: slow red fade pulse (750ms up/down) when empty and parking available;
  solid 75% red (BF0000) while held; fast white flash (200ms on/off) for Goblin
  capture objectives. Held state always overrides availability/objective lighting.
- Empty spots remain available after other balls park while another capture is safe.
  At capacity, empty spots turn off and entering balls return promptly.
- Goblin: during the 10-second challenge, its four qualifying areas flash green
  and each collected area turns off. Other eligible empty saucers pulse red.
- Victory Laps: full GI fade white -> red -> white -> blue -> white, repeating.
