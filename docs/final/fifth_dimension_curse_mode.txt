FIFTH DIMENSION CURSE MINI-WIZARD

STATUS
Implemented. The VUK timing correction requires physical-machine playtesting.

WIZARD INTRO
1. CITY ZONE MULTIBALL
2. KEEP ZONES BRIGHT - DAILY BUGLE JACKPOTS
3. PARK FOR RUBIES - SPINNER LIGHTS ADD-A-BALL

WIZARD SUMMARY
- FIFTH DIMENSION COMPLETE
- JACKPOTS (VUK Zone Jackpots plus Ruby Super Jackpots)
- ADD-A-BALLS
- POINTS

VUK RELEASE BEHAVIOR
- During active gameplay, every Daily Bugle VUK visit uses the shared delayed-release service.
- Both an unlit VUK visit and a Curse Jackpot wait 1.5 seconds before the up-kicker fires.
- The shared service checks the VUK switch after the first pulse and may issue one recovery pulse if the ball remains detected.
- The ball captured to start the mini-wizard remains held throughout the introduction.
- The post-introduction release remains immediate; the new 1.5-second gameplay delay does not add another wait after the intro.

GAMEPLAY SUMMARY
- Fifth Dimension Curse is a three-ball multiball.
- Playfield switches illuminate six temporary city zones.
- Zones remain bright for 5 seconds, flicker, and return to dim after 8 seconds.
- Entering the VUK with active zones collects a Curse Jackpot based on the number of active zones plus the chapter Case File bonus.
- Entering the VUK with no active zones scores no Curse Jackpot and uses the same 1.5-second release.
- Any saucer may park its ball while at least one other ball remains loose. With four balls in play, all three saucers may be occupied.
- Saucer 1 maps to the left upper target, Saucer 2 to center, and Saucer 3 to right.
- After a VUK Zone Jackpot, every occupied saucer lights its matching upper target for a Ruby Super Jackpot.
- Each Ruby Super Jackpot scores 1,000,000 plus the chapter Case File bonus.
- Collecting a Ruby Super turns off that target, holds the parked ball for 2 seconds, then ejects its matching saucer.
- A deadlock guard immediately releases a parked ball if no loose ball remains playable.
- When fewer than four balls are in play, a main-spinner hit randomly lights STAR, upper A, or upper B for add-a-ball.
- Only one add-a-ball target may be active at a time. The main spinner is ignored during its 10-second window.
- Hitting the lit target stops the timer and adds a ball. If the target expires, no ball is added.
- After collection or expiry, the next eligible main-spinner hit may light another random target.
- Add-a-ball remains available throughout the mode, with a maximum of four balls in play.
- The mini-wizard ends when multiball ends.
