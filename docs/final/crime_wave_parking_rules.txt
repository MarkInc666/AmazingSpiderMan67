CRIME WAVE — PARKING RULES

Starts with 3-ball multiball. Up to 3 saucers may hold balls, provided 1
ball remains loose. Normally 2 can park with the 3-ball start.
Independent 15-second holds. Occupied switch chatter does not score, refresh
Doctor Cool, or restart parking timers.
On each drain, check after the game count and ball saves update. Release the
oldest parked ball if none remain loose. A 250ms guard also checks continuously.
Rooftop reward uses its live matching parked-ball 2x bonus, then releases that
matching ball. Other parked balls keep their independent timers.
Mode end releases parked balls in order, staggered by 300ms.

Empty saucers flash white when Doctor Cool is uncaptured or in his final
5-second warning. Otherwise available parking slowly pulses red. Held spots
show solid 75% red (BF0000), overriding objectives. Unavailable ordinary spots
are off. Other villain-area timing, scoring and Super rules are unchanged.
