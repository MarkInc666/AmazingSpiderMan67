SPIDER-SLAYER — HUNT MODE

Build phase
- One random shot category starts lit.
- Successful lit shots add another lit category; earlier lit shots remain active.
- Each logical category has its own repeat-value ladder: 50,000, 25,000,
  10,000, then 5,000 for every later hit.
- Bigger Jackpots changes that ladder to 75,000, 50,000, 25,000, then 10,000.
- More Jackpots preserves the full first-hit value on the second hit: normal
  becomes 50,000 / 50,000 / 10,000 / 5,000; with Bigger Jackpots it becomes
  75,000 / 75,000 / 25,000 / 10,000.
- Hunt Time begins on the first successful lit shot and stops on the fifteenth.
- Shot Assist makes the first successful shot count as two of the fifteen hits,
  lights two additional categories, and awards both that category's first- and
  second-hit values.
- Categories: left web, center web, left sling, right sling, left pop, right pop, left bank, right bank, star, and any saucer.
- Any target or rubber in a bank counts for its bank category. Bank resets remain normal.

Slayer Jackpot
- The fifteenth successful shot opens the rooftop gate and lights the Daily Bugle VUK.
- Base Slayer Jackpot is 2,000,000 and falls by 100,000 per second to a
  100,000 floor while its timer remains active.
- Bigger Jackpots starts it at 2,500,000.
- The VUK is available for 20 seconds, or 25 seconds with More Time. When time
  expires, the jackpot becomes zero, its chase stops, and the rooftop gate closes.
- Safety Net starts a ten-second, one-ball save when the VUK lights.
- Lit hunt categories continue scoring from their individual repeat ladders during
  and after the VUK countdown.
- The display shows both the falling jackpot and seconds remaining.
- Collecting the VUK destroys the Spider-Slayer and ends the mode.

Lighting
- Dark red GI during the hunt.
- Active shots pulse red. Bank categories use bank GI lights.
- The shared saucer category pulses all six red hole lamps; green 1/2/3 inserts stay off.
- Successful shots flash the playfield red.
- At fifteen hits, the active hunt lights remain available, GI changes green, and the shared
  Master Vine route chase runs from the lower spinner through the ramp to the
  Daily Bugle VUK. It stops on the VUK hit or mode cleanup.

Summary
- MODE SCORE
- SLAYER JP (collected value only; zero if uncollected or expired)
- HUNT TIME appears only after the fifteen-hit hunt is completed.
