Lizard Mode: “Serum Chase”

Core rules:
- The rooftop gate stays closed for the entire mode.
- The player gets two serum delivery attempts, or three with More Jackpots.
- Build each serum by hitting the left and right pop bumpers once each.
- An unhit pop ring pulses green; after that pop is hit, its ring stays solid purple.
- When both pops are complete, serum becomes ready and the delivery countdown starts.
- Both deliveries are made at the left web target, which flashes purple.
- During the delivery countdown, the red Star insert flashes as an optional 10X delivery multiplier.
- Hitting the Star arms 10X for that delivery; the Star then stays solid red.
- The 10X applies to whatever delivery value remains when the left web (or Shot Assist delivery) is hit.
- A missed/expired serum consumes that delivery attempt unless Safety Net saves it.

Delivery scoring:
- Completing both pops awards the existing 100,000 serum-build score and starts the delivery.
- Base timer: 16 seconds.
- More Time: 20 seconds.
- Base delivery jackpot: 1,000,000.
- Bigger Jackpots: 1,500,000 starting value.
- Base decay: 100,000 per second.
- More Time decay: 50,000 per second.
- Delivery value cannot fall below 100,000.
- If 10X is armed, the final award is 10 times the value remaining at the instant of delivery.
- Each later attempt starts at the current full serum value and receives a fresh timer.
- A successful serum delivery plays a 2-second white/green left-web success show before the next serum build begins.
- If a delivery timer expires, GI fades to off over 2 seconds. The next serum attempt does not begin until that fade finishes; after the final expired attempt, mode completion also waits for the fade.
- One or more successful deliveries produces the CURED summary. If every attempt expires, the summary reports that Lizard escaped.

Lighting:
- Serum-build GI uses the existing slow F0F040 yellow / F02040 red treatment.
- Unhit pop rings pulse green; completed pop rings are solid purple.
- Active-delivery GI holds teal 406060 for 1.25 seconds, flashes F02040 for 250ms, and repeats every 1.5 seconds.
- The left-web delivery target flashes purple while serum is active.
- During delivery the red Star insert flashes at 250ms on / 250ms off while 10X is available, then goes solid red when 10X is armed.

A+B serum boost:
- The A and B inserts flash yellow while that letter is available and go solid yellow when collected.
- Every A+B completion adds 500,000 to the starting value of all remaining serum attempts.
- If serum is currently active, the same 500,000 is added immediately and the full delivery timer restarts.
- The cumulative A+B increase has no cap and remains active until the mode ends.
- The timer restart plays a short GI celebration, then normal Lizard GI resumes automatically.
- After A+B is completed, both letters reset and flash again for the next A+B cycle.

Case Files:
- More Jackpots: adds a third serum delivery attempt using the same pop-build, Star-10X, and left-web delivery rules.
- Bigger Jackpots: raises the starting delivery value to 1,500,000.
- More Time: adds 4 seconds and slows decay to 50,000 per second.
- Safety Net: saves the first expired serum.
- Shot Assist: during an active delivery, the main-playfield spinner or the left B rollover counts as the left-web delivery. The upper trispinner does not count.
