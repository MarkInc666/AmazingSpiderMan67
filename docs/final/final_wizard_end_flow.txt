FINAL WIZARD END FLOW
=====================

1. Final Showdown multiball ends.
2. final_showdown_mode_complete is posted and the current player is marked retired.
3. At that moment, snapshot future balls:
   unused normal balls + queued earned extra balls.
4. Flippers/autofire are disabled immediately and the remaining physical ball drains naturally.
5. The Kingpin / Final Showdown summary may run during that drain; shutdown does not wait for it.
6. Once the ball drains, normal ball_ending Bonus runs.
7. Hold Bonus is resolved as an effective last-ball award.
8. BALLS REMAINING awards 10,000,000 per snapshotted future ball.
9. TOTAL BONUS is shown.
10. A five-second YOU'VE REACHED THE END / KINGPIN DEFEATED screen shows the retired player's final score.
11. Bonus finishes and queued extra balls for the retired player are consumed.
12. If another non-retired player has a normal ball remaining, MPF rotates and player_retirement skips retired players.
13. If nobody remains eligible, player_retirement calls game.end_game(), preserving MPF's normal game_will_end, game_ending, high-score, game_ended, and attract flow.

September 9, 2026 correction: player_retirement is now a persistent whole-game
controller (game_mode false, stop_on_ball_end false). The earlier configuration
stopped it automatically after Ball 1, preventing the retirement snapshot and
remaining-ball award during later balls.
