THE SPIDER-MEN — ALIGN THE HOMEWORLD RAY

STATUS

Coded. Needs on-machine playtest.


CORE RULES

The mode uses the same six switches as the normal skill shot:

- Saucer 1
- Saucer 2
- Saucer 3
- Star rollover
- A rollover
- B rollover

All six positions begin pulsing red. The three saucers use their paired red
hole lamps; the green numbered 1/2/3 inserts are not used. Star, A and B use
their normal inserts and are also driven red.

Left and right flipper presses rotate the entire six-position light pattern.
Completed and incomplete states move together. Hitting a pulsing position
converts it to solid red. Hitting a solid position scores a Fine Adjustment but
does not advance the alignment.

The first Jackpot starts the proton countdown. Complete all six positions before
the countdown expires to align the Homeworld Ray and send the Spider-Men home.
The rooftop gate is closed for the entire mode because all six shots are on the
main playfield.


TIMER

- The first Jackpot starts a 20-second shot timer (30 seconds with More Time).
- Every subsequent hit on any of the six alignment positions restarts the entire
  20/30-second timer, including hits on already-solid/unlit positions.
- Flipper rotations alone do not reset the timer.
- If the timer expires before all six positions are aligned, the mode fails.
- The alignment timer stops when the sixth position is completed.


SCORING

- Each of the first five newly aligned positions: 250,000 Jackpot.
- With Bigger Jackpots: 375,000 per normal alignment.
- Sixth position: 500,000 Jackpot (750,000 with Bigger Jackpots).
- Already-solid/unlit position: 50,000 Fine Adjustment, and refresh shot timer.
- More Jackpots center-web Super: 1,000,000, or 1,500,000 with Bigger Jackpots.
- Normal clean six-position total: 1,750,000 points, excluding starting award.
- Shot Assist's free position awards its full corresponding alignment value.
  The Super counts toward Mode Points, not the regular Jackpot count.


CASE FILES

- More Jackpots: after six alignments, light center web for the Super Jackpot
  (1,000,000, or 1,500,000 with Bigger Jackpots) for 20 seconds. Collecting
  or letting the window expire completes the mode.
- Bigger Jackpots: first five alignments 375,000 each; final 750,000;
  optional Super 1,500,000.
- More Time: increase the resetting alignment shot timer from 20 to 30 seconds.
- Safety Net: retain the shared opening Case File ball save.
- Shot Assist: first completed pulsing position also completes one additional
  random uncompleted position and awards its corresponding Jackpot value.


LIGHTING

Alignment lights pulse/hold red. The full playfield GI uses the shared physical
height bands 1-8 as a continuous bottom-to-top chase. All bands use soft blue
8080F0 while a subtler three-band A090E8/B090D8/A090E8 wave advances through the bands
every 250ms, wrapping around at the ends, and then repeats.

During the optional Super window, the six positions remain solid red, the
soft-blue/muted-lavender band chase continues, and the center web flashes red. A shared
white approach chase also runs from Multibonus through Advance Multiplier and
Special to the center-web insert. The approach chase stops immediately when
the Super is collected, the window expires, or the mode stops.


SUMMARY
