KINGPIN: FINAL SHOWDOWN

OVERVIEW

Final Showdown starts as a 2-ball multiball with a 20 second opening ball save.
The wizard contains five recap phases followed by the Kingpin lock phase.

The five recap phases are:
- Timed Collect
- Staged Drops
- Rooftop Build
- Area Control
- Reveal & Collect

Timed Collect is always the opening phase. During every recap phase, each hit on
the main/lower spinner advances the NEXT PHASE pointer to the next unplayed
phase. When the current phase ends, the phase under that pointer starts next.
Played phases are skipped. After all five recap phases have played, Final Lock
starts automatically.

Final Showdown is a single continuous wizard attempt. Dropping from multiball to
one ball does NOT end the wizard; the recap phases continue on the last live ball.
The recap sequence ends only when Kingpin is exposed after all five phases, or
when every live ball has actually drained.


KINGPIN BANK

Every phase Jackpot or Super Jackpot is scored immediately and is also added to
the KINGPIN BANK. Incidental phase scoring is not added to the bank.

Examples of scoring that DOES NOT enter the Kingpin Bank:
- Timed Collect 100K shots
- Staged Drop 250K target hits
- Area Control 100K upper-spinner spins
- Reveal phase 50K drop-target hits

The final Kingpin shot scores the complete accumulated KINGPIN BANK again.


ADD-A-BALL

A+B qualifies ADD-A-BALL.

A may be completed by either A rollover.
B may be completed by either B rollover.

When A+B is complete:
- ADD-A-BALL remains ready until collected.
- The 1, 2 and 3 inserts indicate ADD-A-BALL READY.
- Any saucer collects the add-a-ball.
- The new ball is served to the shooter lane.
- Each added ball receives the Final Showdown add-a-ball ball save.
- Maximum balls in play: 4.
- If already at 4 balls, the qualification remains banked until a ball is lost.
- After an add-a-ball is collected, A+B resets and can be completed again.

Outside Final Lock, surplus balls may park in a saucer for 20 seconds before
being ejected. Phase-specific saucer rules override the normal parking behavior.


PHASE 1 - TIMED COLLECT

Duration: 20 seconds.

Uses the same repeatable shot pool as DeVargas / City of Gold:
- Left web target
- Center web target
- Left pop
- Right pop
- Left bank
- Right bank
- Upper A
- Upper B
- Middle A
- Middle B
- Upper targets
- Main/lower spinner
- Upper spinner

One available shot is added every second. Lit shots remain available until
collected or until the 20 second window ends. A collected shot may be selected
again later in the same phase.

Each collected timed shot scores 100,000.

At the end of 20 seconds all remaining timed shots clear and both web targets
light for 20 seconds for the SUPER JACKPOT.

SUPER JACKPOT = 1,000,000 + (250,000 x timed shots collected).

Either web target collects the Super Jackpot. The collected Super is added to
the Kingpin Bank. If the 20 second web-target window expires, the Super is lost and the next recap phase begins.

Upper-playfield timed opportunities open the rooftop gate as needed.


PHASE 2 - STAGED DROPS

Three precision stages are played.

For each stage:
1. Both drop banks reset.
2. The game programmatically drops every target except one selected standing
   target.
3. The player gets one attempt.
4. Hitting the standing target is a success and scores 250,000.
5. Hitting that bank's associated rear rubber before the target is a miss.
6. The game resets and stages another single standing target.

Programmatic knockdowns do not count as player hits.

After all three stages, the number of successful target hits sets the multiplier
for the next Jackpot:

0 successes = 1X
1 success   = 2X
2 successes = 3X
3 successes = 5X

Base Jackpot = 1,000,000.

Both web targets then light for 20 seconds. Either web target collects:

0 successes = 1,000,000
1 success   = 2,000,000
2 successes = 3,000,000
3 successes = 5,000,000

The collected Jackpot is added to the Kingpin Bank. If the web-target window
times out, the Jackpot is lost and the next recap phase begins.


PHASE 3 - ROOFTOP BUILD

The rooftop gate opens. The build window begins when a ball enters the rooftop.

The player has 20 presses of the upper-right flipper. After the 20th press the
upper flippers disable until the rooftop visit ends.

During the build window:
- Each upper-spinner spin adds 100,000 to the common exit Jackpot base.
- Each upper-left target hit increases the LEFT exit multiplier by one step.
- Each upper-center target hit increases the CENTER exit multiplier by one step.
- Each upper-right target hit increases the RIGHT exit multiplier by one step.

Each exit begins at 1X before its target has been hit:
0 target hits = 1X
1 target hit  = 2X
2 target hits = 3X
3 target hits = 4X
...and so on.

Exit Jackpot formula:

upper-spinner spins x 100,000 x (matching upper-target hits + 1)

The Jackpot is collected at the exit actually taken:
- Left upper exit = LEFT Jackpot
- Right upper exit = RIGHT Jackpot
- Center exit is inferred by the established right-bank upper rubber or
  middle-A switch = CENTER Jackpot

The collected exit Jackpot is added to the Kingpin Bank and the phase ends.


PHASE 4 - AREA CONTROL

LOWER PLAYFIELD

The player has 25 seconds to control all four lower areas.
Completed areas remain complete for the rest of this phase.

Left area:
- Complete the left drop bank
- Hit the left web target

Right area:
- Complete the right drop bank
- Hit the right-bank rubber

Center area:
- Hit the left pop
- Hit the right pop
- Hit the center web target

Slingshot area:
- Hit the left sling
- Hit the right sling

If all four lower areas are not completed within 25 seconds, Area Control ends
without its rooftop opportunity and the next recap phase begins.

ROOFTOP

When all four lower areas are controlled, the rooftop gate opens.
The rooftop timer begins only when a ball enters the rooftop.

The player then has 20 seconds.

The first hit on any upper target completes the fifth/rooftop area and enables
the upper spinner for scoring.

After the rooftop target is hit:
- Each upper-spinner spin scores 100,000.

At the end of the 20 second rooftop window:
- The upper flippers disable.
- The rooftop gate closes.
- The phase ends.

The 100K spinner awards are regular phase scoring and are not added to the
Kingpin Bank.


PHASE 5 - REVEAL & COLLECT

Every drop-target hit scores 50,000.

Before the first saucer collect, completing either drop bank reveals one
additional saucer, up to all three.

Flipper buttons rotate the lit/unlit saucer pattern throughout the phase.
During the reveal portion this lets the player position an UNLIT safe saucer
where an accidental ball is likely to settle.

Reveal states:
- 1 saucer revealed: 1 lit / 2 unlit
- 2 saucers revealed: 2 lit / 1 unlit
- 3 saucers revealed: all 3 lit

The first lit saucer collected ends further revealing, but already-revealed
saucer awards remain collectible. Flipper cycling continues during collection,
allowing the player to move the remaining lit positions toward the ball.

Saucer Jackpot value is fixed by how many saucers had been revealed when the
first lit saucer was collected:

1 revealed = 1,000,000 per collected saucer
2 revealed = 2,000,000 per collected saucer
3 revealed = 3,000,000 per collected saucer

Therefore revealing and collecting all three can award up to 9,000,000.
Each saucer Jackpot is added to the Kingpin Bank.

An unlit saucer does not collect a Reveal Jackpot and follows normal 20-second
parking behavior.

The phase ends after all saucer awards that were revealed before the first
collect have been collected.


FINAL LOCK - KINGPIN

After all five recap phases have played, Final Lock begins.

Normal playfield scoring remains active. The rooftop gate stays CLOSED until
all three saucers are filled.

A+B add-a-ball remains available throughout Final Lock.

Balls entering the three saucers become permanent Final Lock balls instead of
20-second parked balls. If a saucer is already occupied when Final Lock starts,
that occupied ball becomes a Final Lock ball immediately.

Objective:
- Lock one ball in saucer 1.
- Lock one ball in saucer 2.
- Lock one ball in saucer 3.
- Keep one additional ball free on the playfield.

When all three saucers are filled and a free fourth ball remains:
- The rooftop gate opens.
- The Daily Bugle VUK becomes the Kingpin shot.
- The three locked saucer balls are released one at a time, two seconds apart.
- The gate stays open and the VUK remains qualified throughout the release.

Once Kingpin is exposed, normal multiball-ended handling is overridden. The
player only needs to keep at least one live ball and shoot the Daily Bugle VUK.

If play drains to the last live ball and the player still has an unused game
ball remaining, a dedicated only-last-ball save returns a ball to the shooter
lane. That save consumes one unused game ball but does not tear down Final
Showdown or reset the Kingpin Bank. The player continues in the same final VUK
shot state.

When no unused game balls remain, that final-shot save is disabled. Losing the
last live ball then ends Final Showdown in defeat: KINGPIN GETS AWAY.

Before Kingpin is exposed, a safety rule prevents every physical ball from
remaining parked in the saucers with no free ball available: one lock is
released if necessary.

Shoot the Daily Bugle VUK after Kingpin is exposed to defeat Kingpin.

FINAL KINGPIN AWARD = complete accumulated KINGPIN BANK.

That award is scored in addition to all phase Jackpots when they were originally
collected.

Successful Kingpin collection completes Final Showdown and follows the existing
per-player Final Wizard completion/retirement flow. Any unused game balls still
remaining at that moment are consumed and award 50,000,000 each as a single
BALLS REMAINING completion award; those unused balls are not played and do not
run separate normal bonus counts.

Before Kingpin is exposed, MPF's normal multiball-ended event does not end Final
Showdown when play drops to one ball. The recap phases continue until either
Kingpin is exposed or the final live ball actually drains. During the final VUK
state, the conditional last-ball save described above may consume remaining game
balls to keep the same Final Showdown attempt alive. A failed Final Showdown ends
that player's game through the same retirement/end-of-game flow as a successful
final Kingpin shot, but awards no remaining-ball completion bonus.


PHASE MISSES / GAME END
------------------------
Missing a recap-phase objective does NOT end Final Showdown. The missed Jackpot
or Super Jackpot is simply lost and the mode advances to the next unplayed
phase. This includes:
- Timed Collect Super timing out.
- Staged Drops Jackpot timing out.
- Rooftop Build exiting with no spinner value built.
- Area Control failing to finish the four lower areas in 25 seconds.
- Area Control reaching the roof but failing to hit an upper target in 20 seconds.

Dropping to one ball before Kingpin is exposed does NOT end Final Showdown. The
current recap phase continues on that ball. If the last live ball drains before
Kingpin is exposed, Final Showdown ends in defeat. Once the final Kingpin VUK
shot is active, the conditional last-ball save may consume an unused game ball
to continue the final shot; with none remaining, that last-ball drain is defeat.
