KINGPIN: FINAL SHOWDOWN

OVERVIEW

Final Showdown starts as a 2-ball multiball with a 20 second opening ball save.
It contains five recap phases. A phase is only removed from the selection pool
when it is successfully completed. Failed phases remain unfinished and may be
selected again later.

The five recap phases are:
- Timed Collect
- Staged Drops
- Rooftop Build
- Area Control
- Reveal & Collect

Timed Collect is always the opening phase. During every recap phase, each hit on
the main/lower spinner advances the NEXT PHASE pointer through the unfinished
phases other than the one currently being played. When the current phase ends,
the phase under that pointer starts next. If only the current phase remains
unfinished, a failure immediately repeats it.

Once all five recap phases have been completed successfully, the rooftop gate
opens and the Daily Bugle VUK becomes the final Kingpin shot.

Dropping from multiball to one ball does not end Final Showdown. If every live
ball drains before the final Kingpin shot is qualified, Kingpin gets away and
the player's game ends.


KINGPIN BANK

Phase Jackpots and Super Jackpots are scored immediately and are also added to
the KINGPIN BANK. Incidental scoring is not added unless a phase specifically
says that it is banked.

The final Daily Bugle VUK shot scores the entire accumulated KINGPIN BANK again.


ADD-A-BALL

A+B qualifies ADD-A-BALL.

A may be completed by either A rollover.
B may be completed by either B rollover.

When A+B is complete:
- ADD-A-BALL remains ready until collected.
- The 1, 2 and 3 inserts indicate ADD-A-BALL READY.
- Any saucer collects the add-a-ball.
- The new ball is served to the shooter lane.
- Each added ball receives the Final Showdown add-a-ball ball save.
- Maximum balls in play: 4.
- If already at 4 balls, the qualification remains banked until a ball is lost.
- After an add-a-ball is collected, A+B resets and can be completed again.

During recap phases, surplus balls may park in a saucer for 12 seconds before
being ejected. Reveal & Collect overrides normal saucer parking while its saucer
awards are active.


PHASE 1 - TIMED COLLECT

Duration: 20 seconds.

Uses the same repeatable shot pool as DeVargas / City of Gold:
- Left web target
- Center web target
- Left pop
- Right pop
- Left bank
- Right bank
- Upper A
- Upper B
- Middle A
- Middle B
- Upper targets
- Main/lower spinner
- Upper spinner

One available shot is added every second. Lit shots remain available until
collected or until the 20 second window ends. Each collected shot scores
100,000.

At the end of the 20 second window:
- If zero shots were collected, the phase fails immediately and no Super lights.
- If one or more shots were collected, both web targets light for 20 seconds.

SUPER JACKPOT = 1,000,000 + (250,000 x timed shots collected).

Either web target collects the Super Jackpot. The collected Super is added to
the Kingpin Bank and the phase succeeds. If the 20 second web-target window
expires, the phase fails and remains unfinished.

Upper-playfield timed opportunities open the rooftop gate as needed.


PHASE 2 - STAGED DROPS

Four precision rounds are played.

For each round:
1. Both drop banks reset.
2. The game programmatically drops every target except one selected standing
   target.
3. The player gets one attempt.
4. Hitting the standing target is a success and scores 250,000.
5. Hitting that bank's associated rear rubber before the target is a miss.
6. The game resets and stages another single standing target.

Programmatic knockdowns do not count as player hits.

After all four rounds, successful target hits set the multiplier for a
1,000,000 base Jackpot:

0 successes = 0X -- phase fails immediately and no Jackpot lights
1 success   = 2X = 2,000,000
2 successes = 4X = 4,000,000
3 successes = 6X = 6,000,000
4 successes = 8X = 8,000,000

With at least one successful round, both web targets light for 20 seconds.
Either web target collects the Jackpot, adds it to the Kingpin Bank, and
completes the phase. If the web-target window expires, the phase fails and
remains unfinished.


PHASE 3 - ROOFTOP BUILD

The rooftop gate opens. The build window begins when a ball enters the rooftop.

The player has 20 presses of the upper-right flipper. After the 20th press the
upper flipper disables until the rooftop visit ends.

During the build window:
- Each upper-spinner spin adds 100,000 to the common exit Jackpot base.
- Each upper-left target hit increases the LEFT exit multiplier by one step.
- Each upper-center target hit increases the CENTER exit multiplier by one step.
- Each upper-right target hit increases the RIGHT exit multiplier by one step.

Each exit begins at 1X before its target has been hit:
0 target hits = 1X
1 target hit  = 2X
2 target hits = 3X
3 target hits = 4X
...and so on.

Exit Jackpot formula:

upper-spinner spins x 100,000 x (matching upper-target hits + 1)

The Jackpot is collected at the exit actually taken:
- Left upper exit = LEFT Jackpot
- Right upper exit = RIGHT Jackpot
- Center exit is inferred by the established right-bank upper rubber or
  middle-A switch = CENTER Jackpot

The phase succeeds only if the rooftop visit included:
- at least one upper-spinner spin, and
- at least one upper-target hit.

If either requirement is missing when the ball exits, the phase fails and
remains unfinished. A successful exit Jackpot is added to the Kingpin Bank.


PHASE 4 - AREA CONTROL

LOWER PLAYFIELD

The player has 25 seconds to control all four lower areas. Completed areas stay
complete for that attempt.

Left area:
- Complete the left drop bank
- Hit the left web target

Right area:
- Complete the right drop bank
- Hit the right-bank rubber

Center area:
- Hit the left pop
- Hit the right pop
- Hit the center web target

Slingshot area:
- Hit the left sling
- Hit the right sling

If all four lower areas are not completed within 25 seconds, the phase fails
and remains unfinished.

ROOFTOP

When all four lower areas are controlled, the rooftop gate opens. The rooftop
20-second timer begins when a ball enters the rooftop.

The first hit on any upper target completes the fifth/rooftop area. If no upper
target is hit before the 20-second rooftop timer expires, the phase fails.

After the rooftop target has been hit:
- Each upper-spinner spin scores 100,000.

When the 20-second rooftop timer expires:
- The upper flipper disables.
- The rooftop gate closes.
- The phase succeeds.
- The total 100,000-per-spin value earned during this rooftop period is added
  to the Kingpin Bank. It is not scored a second time when banked.


PHASE 5 - REVEAL & COLLECT

Every drop-target hit scores 50,000.

There are six possible revealed collect shots:
- Left saucer
- Center saucer
- Right saucer
- Star rollover
- Upper A
- Upper B

Completing either drop bank reveals one additional shot, up to all six.
Flipper buttons rotate the lit/unlit pattern across all six possible collect
positions.

A 20-second reveal timer begins when the phase starts. Each new bank completion
that reveals another shot resets this timer to 20 seconds.

Before the first collect, flipper cycling can be used defensively to move unlit
safe positions toward the ball. The first lit-shot collect ends further
revealing and starts the collect portion.

The value of every collect is fixed by how many shots had been revealed when
the first collect was made:

1 revealed = 1,000,000 each
2 revealed = 2,000,000 each
3 revealed = 3,000,000 each
4 revealed = 4,000,000 each
5 revealed = 5,000,000 each
6 revealed = 6,000,000 each

Each collected award is scored immediately and added to the Kingpin Bank.

After the first collect, a 20-second collect timer runs. Every additional
successful collect resets that timer to 20 seconds. Flipper cycling remains
active so the remaining lit awards can be repositioned.

Success condition: at least one revealed shot is collected.

- If the reveal timer expires before any collect, the phase fails.
- Once at least one shot has been collected, the phase is successful.
- If all revealed awards are collected, the phase ends immediately.
- Otherwise, when the 20-second collect timer expires, the phase ends
  successfully and any remaining revealed awards are lost.


FINAL KINGPIN SHOT

After all five recap phases have been completed successfully:
- The rooftop gate opens immediately.
- The Daily Bugle VUK lights as the final Kingpin shot.
- No saucer-lock or parking requirement is used.
- Any balls already parked in saucers are released.
- Multiball may continue with any number of balls, or naturally collapse to a
  single live ball.
- A+B add-a-ball remains available until Kingpin is defeated.

Shoot the Daily Bugle VUK to defeat Kingpin.

The final shot awards the entire accumulated KINGPIN BANK again and completes
the player's game.

If the final shot reaches the last live ball, a dedicated only-last-ball save
may consume one unused future game ball and return a replacement to the shooter
lane. Normal remaining game balls are consumed first, then earned extra balls.
The final-shot state and Kingpin Bank remain intact through these retries.

If no future game balls remain and the final live ball drains before the VUK is
made, KINGPIN GETS AWAY and the player's game ends in defeat.

On successful Kingpin defeat, any still-unused future normal balls or earned
extra balls are worth 50,000,000 each. Those balls are consumed as a single
remaining-balls completion award; they are not played and do not receive normal
end-of-ball bonus counts.
