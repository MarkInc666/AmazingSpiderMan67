HARLEY CLIVENDON MODE — TWO-ZONE SUPER / TIMED HOLD
===================================================

Implementation status
---------------------
Updated against ASM67_2026_11_16_8pm plus the 20-second hold and grey GI
lighting patches. Physical-machine testing is still required.

Core rules
----------
- Two-ball multiball begins with the existing opening ball save.
- At mode start, NO zones are available or flashing. Saucer lamps guide a lock.
- Capturing a ball in a saucer scores 50K and starts a 20-second hold.
- Only while a ball is actually held may unqualified zones be collected.
- With a ball held, available zones pulse grey 808080 <-> C0C0C0.
- Each distinct zone hit scores 50K and stays solid blue until the Super
  Jackpot is collected, INCLUDING after saucer ejection or timeout.
- TWO qualified zones light the Daily Bugle VUK Super Jackpot. Once ready,
  it remains collectable with BOTH balls free, even after the hold expires.
- Extra zone collections before the Super increase its value (100K per area,
  or 150K with Bigger Jackpots).
- Collecting the VUK Super Jackpot clears ALL qualified zones. The next
  build requires another ball in a saucer; no areas become available on
  their own between locks.
- A saucer hold expires after 20 seconds. Hitting a second different saucer
  during a hold ejects that ball and restarts the held-ball timer. A repeat
  event from the occupied saucer also restarts the timer; the hardware
  cannot distinguish a true second ball from switch bounce in that case.
- After the VUK Super, the held saucer ball is released and another lock
  becomes available after the established delay.

Zone lights
-----------
- General illumination: steady 808080.
- Available: pulsing between 808080 and C0C0C0 while a ball is held.
- Qualified: steady blue until a Super Jackpot is collected.
- Unqualified and not currently lock-active: no zone show (background GI).
- Red saucer lamps indicate availability or the held saucer.
- VUK/spinner ready light: yellow pulse.

Case Files
----------
- More Jackpots: after collecting a Super, the NEXT saucer lock spots
  one randomly selected zone automatically. This replaces retaining two
  zones, which would now instantly requalify the two-zone Super.
- Bigger Jackpots: 150K per qualified area instead of 100K.
- More Time: existing extended opening ball save.
- Safety Net: existing ball save after the first VUK Super.
- Shot Assist: first manually collected area also spots another unlit area.

Recovery and testing
--------------------
Check timeout with one qualified zone (blue persists; unqualified areas
stop pulsing), timeout with two qualified zones (VUK remains ready with
both balls free), second-saucer hold restart, Jackpot clearing all zones,
and new lock re-enabling only unqualified zone pulses. Test More Jackpots
and Shot Assist together. The 20-second release is a fallback for a ball
physically trapped behind an occupied saucer; the switch itself cannot
reliably detect two balls at one saucer.
