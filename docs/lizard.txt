Lizard Mode: “Serum Chase”

Core rules:
- The rooftop gate stays closed for the entire mode.
- The player gets exactly two serum delivery attempts.
- During serum collection, GI is red and the red star rollover flashes.
- Hitting the star immediately starts the delivery timer and changes GI to green.
- Both deliveries are made at the left web target, which flashes purple.
- A missed/expired serum consumes that delivery attempt.

Delivery scoring:
- Base timer: 16 seconds.
- More Time: 20 seconds.
- Base delivery jackpot: 1,000,000.
- Bigger Jackpots: 1,500,000 starting value.
- Base decay: 100,000 per second.
- More Time decay: 50,000 per second.
- Delivery value cannot fall below 100,000.
- The second attempt resets to the full starting value and a fresh timer.

Case Files:
- More Jackpots: after a successful left-web delivery, the center web flashes purple for an extra jackpot worth 50% of the collected value. It remains available for 10 seconds or until the star is hit to begin the next serum attempt.
- Bigger Jackpots: raises the starting delivery value to 1,500,000.
- More Time: adds 4 seconds and slows decay to 50,000 per second.
- Safety Net: saves the first expired serum.
- Shot Assist: during either active delivery, the main-playfield spinner or the left B rollover counts as the left-web delivery. The upper trispinner does not count.
