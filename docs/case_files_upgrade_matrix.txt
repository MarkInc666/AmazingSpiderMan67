Rules and scope

- This matrix contains exactly the 55 active regular villains defined by villain_progression.py.
- Regular villains may use five individual powers: More Jackpots (MJ), Bigger Jackpots (BJ), More Time (MT), Safety Net (SN), and Shot Assist (SA).

- Chapter mini-wizards do not receive those five powers. Every mini-wizard Jackpot and Super Jackpot instead adds 20K for each Case File collected in that chapter (0–25 files; 0–500K). Ordinary switch, spinner, target, progress, and other non-jackpot scoring is unchanged.
