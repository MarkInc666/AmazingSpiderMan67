SHARED MODE STATUS WIDGET

The persistent mode-status widget is shared by all modes through
widgets/mode_status.tscn.

LAYOUT

- The panel is 720 pixels wide and centered near the bottom of the display.
- It fits inside the 740-pixel gap between the Player 3 and Player 4 panels,
  leaving 10 pixels of clearance on each side.
- The status title occupies a centered full-width upper row.
- The status value occupies a centered full-width lower row.
- Title size is 30 pixels.
- Value size is 40 pixels.
- Existing colors, outlines, variable names, and mode-provided text are unchanged.

The stacked layout gives longer values, including multi-area shot status, enough
horizontal room without competing with the title.

TESTING

Check the widget with both short and long status strings. In particular, verify
Parafino, Master Technician, Cyclops, Igor, Magneto, and Professor Pretorius on
the real display and adjust only the shared scene if final cabinet overscan
requires additional padding.
