DR. MANTA — VUK-TO-SAUCER RELAY
================================

STATUS
------
Implemented from the finalized August 5, 2026 design. Needs on-machine playtest.

CORE FLOW
---------
1. Start with one ball. The rooftop gate opens and the main spinner / access GI
   flash to lead the player to the VUK.
2. Shoot the VUK for 100,000. Ball 1 remains physically held in the VUK.
3. A second ball is served with a 10-second ball save.
4. Lock Ball 2 in any saucer for 150,000. The saucer keeps Ball 2 held.
5. Ball 1 is kicked from the VUK to the upper playfield and the 20-second
   Mountain Monster attack begins.
6. Either spinner increases the jackpot by 50,000 per spin. The jackpot begins
   at 100,000 and is capped at 1,000,000.
7. Any upper target collects the current jackpot. The value does not reset.
8. The timer continues even if the ball leaves the rooftop.
9. At zero, jackpot scoring stops and ALL flippers disable immediately.
10. When the active ball reaches the trough, the mode ends and all flippers
    re-enable. The continuing ball stays parked in its saucer throughout the
    villain summary, then releases through the shared occupancy-checked saucer
    cleanup when the summary finishes or is skipped.

The mode is tracked only as PLAYED for chapter progression. Jackpot count is
used for score, status and summary presentation; it does not determine whether
chapter progression advances.

CASE FILES
----------
More Jackpots
  Each spinner spin adds 75,000 instead of 50,000. Cap remains 1,000,000.

Bigger Jackpots
  VUK = 150,000; saucer = 200,000; upper jackpot begins at 200,000.
  Spinner increase and 1,000,000 cap are unchanged.

More Time
  Mountain Monster attack lasts 28 seconds instead of 20.

Safety Net
  Second-ball save lasts 25 seconds instead of 10.

Shot Assist
  The saucer holding Ball 2 maps to the corresponding upper target:
    Saucer 1 -> left upper target
    Saucer 2 -> center upper target
    Saucer 3 -> right upper target
  That target scores 3X the current jackpot once. It may be collected at any
  point during the timed phase and then returns to normal jackpot value.

LIGHTING
--------
Base GI: ocean blue 2060A8.

Phase 1 — Shoot VUK
  Gate open. Main spinner insert flashes white. Spinner GI and upper-access GI
  pulse blue toward the VUK. Saucers and upper targets remain off.

Phase 2 — Lock Ball 2
  VUK path show stops. All three red-lens saucer pairs flash red. Once captured,
  only the occupied saucer pair remains solid red.

Phase 3 — Mountain Monster
  All upper targets flash blue-white. Spinner insert / GI flash to show value
  building. Rooftop GI gives a brief blue-white flash on spinner increases and
  jackpots. With Shot Assist, the target corresponding to the held saucer uses
  a faster white-blue flash until its one-time 3X jackpot is collected.

Final five seconds
  GI alternates ocean blue and cool white.

Timeout
  Upper targets and spinner indications stop. GI falls to dim blue. All flippers
  stay disabled until the active ball reaches the trough.

STATE / SUMMARY DATA
--------------------
The live phase, jackpot value, jackpot count, biggest jackpot, attack timer,
held saucer and Shot Assist availability are mode-local Python state. They are
not persisted as player variables.

Persistent/shared player data is limited to:
- dr_manta_state for villain progression.
- dr_manta_second_ball_save_seconds because MPF YAML reads it for the dynamic
  second-ball-save duration.
- active_mode_points, active_mode_stat_1 and active_mode_stat_2 for the shared
  end-of-mode summary.

PLAYTEST CHECKLIST
------------------
- VUK reliably holds Ball 1 while the second ball serves.
- Second-ball save is 10 seconds normally and 25 with Safety Net.
- Unqualified saucers eject normally; the first qualified saucer holds Ball 2.
- VUK eject reaches the rooftop after the saucer settles.
- Both spinner switches increase jackpot only during the attack.
- Upper targets award current value without resetting it.
- More Jackpots changes each spin increase to 75,000.
- Bigger Jackpots applies 150K / 200K / 200K start values.
- Shot Assist maps saucer 1/2/3 to left/center/right and scores 3X only once.
- All flippers disable exactly at timeout, even if the ball has left the roof.
- Trough arrival ends gameplay and re-enables all flippers, but the held saucer
  does not eject until the villain summary finishes or is skipped.
- Summary completion releases only the held saucer through the shared staggered
  cleanup without double-ejecting either held device.
- Losing Ball 2 after the ball save but before a saucer lock releases the VUK
  ball and ends the mode cleanly.
