DESPERADO — RIGHT-BANK PURSUIT

Complete all five unique right-bank targets before the 60-second pursuit
expires. Round 1 allows one right-bank hit before Desperado escapes and the
bank resets. Each later round allows one additional hit, up to five hits.

New unique targets score 200K. Previously completed targets score 50K and
still consume one of the round's allowed hits. Completing the left 3-bank adds
10 seconds and resets that bank.

Incomplete right-bank inserts pulse bright/dim green through their physical
green lenses. Completed targets remain solid green across later bank resets.
The status display shows the round, seconds remaining, unique targets found,
and hits used in the current round.

The rooftop gate closes when gameplay begins. An unexpected ball entering the
Daily Bugle VUK is immediately ejected.

CASE FILES

- More Time: start with 70 seconds.
- Safety Net: start the 10-second Case File ball save.
- Shot Assist: the first right-bank hit in round 2 spots another unique target.
