ASM67 JACKPOT PRESENTATION AND AUDIO POLICY

- show_mode_jackpot is reserved for an award that scores a Jackpot or Super
  Jackpot immediately.
- play_mode_jackpot and play_mode_super_jackpot are posted locally by the mode
  beside the matching scored award.
- Building or displaying a future Jackpot value uses show_mode_message.
- Jackpot lit, ready, qualification, Add-a-Ball, completion, consolation,
  ordinary bonus, and ordinary objective-hit messages do not use the Jackpot
  presentation or Jackpot audio unless that same action also collects a scored
  Jackpot.
- One collected award posts its matching Jackpot sound exactly once.
