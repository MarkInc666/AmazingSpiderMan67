CHAPTER 8 — ELEMENTAL CHAOS VILLAIN DESIGNS
============================================

CLIVE AND BLOTTO — DESIGNED / NOT CODED
- Blotto Meter starts at 75% and rises every two seconds.
- Spinner hits lower the meter and pause its growth for two seconds.
- When the meter fills, a clean playfield area becomes Blotto'd.
- Any switch hit in that area clears it.
- Win when no areas are Blotto'd and the meter reaches zero.
- Exact area map, scoring and Case File effects still need final workshop.

DOCTOR ZAPP — CODED / NEEDS PLAYTEST
- One hit from each lower bank permanently opens rooftop access.
- Spinner spins score 50K and add one camera flash plus one for each unique
  upper target collected.
- First-time upper targets score 50K and permanently improve flashes per spin.
- Reach 25 flashes to defeat Zapp.
- More Jackpots gives a ten-second points-only spinner window.
- After the objective/window, base mode owns a six-second upper-flipper lockout.
- See doctor_zapp_mode.txt for the complete design.

BOLTON AND BOOMER — DESIGNED / NOT CODED
- Repeating two-ball multiball loop.
- Hit a random lit target, lock one ball in any saucer, then shoot the VUK Super.
- The first Super is untimed; later Supers use a 20-second timer and reset the
  loop if missed.
- Ball-control details, values and Case File effects still need final workshop.

THE SNOWMAN — CODED / NEEDS PLAYTEST (CURRENT CODE)
- Either web begins the copper-wire run.
- Hit the opposite web within 16 seconds; repeated starting-web hits score 25K
  and reset the timer. Shot Assist lets that repeated hit complete the wire.
- First spinner spin defeats Snowman and begins a 10-second bonus-spin window.
- More Time changes the windows to 20 and 16 seconds.
- More Jackpots increases each following spin by 25K.
- See snowman_mode.txt for the complete design.

THE PLUTONIANS — CODED / NEEDS PLAYTEST (CURRENT CODE)
- Six Super Swami areas begin frozen and rooftop access stays open.
- One hit thaws a frozen area at any time.
- The active Freeze Ray fires every 12 seconds and randomly refreezes one
  thawed area.
- Upper targets block/reset the ray for 20 seconds, or 25 with More Time.
- Thaw all six simultaneously to repair the Space Warp Control.
- More Jackpots awards one upper-target jackpot per roof entrance.
- See plutonians_mode.txt for the complete design.
