# GREEN GOBLIN — CHAOS MULTIBALL

## Start

- Start two-ball Chaos Multiball.
- Chaos Bonus starts at 250K.
- Chaos Bonus persists throughout the mode and is clamped between 50K and 1M.
- Goblin Bonus starts at 0 and has no cap.
- The mode ends when multiball returns to one ball.

## Unsafe Chaos

- GI is red.
- All six shot areas are solid:
  - Star
  - Pops
  - Left web
  - Right web
  - Left drops
  - Right drops
- Saucers remain lit.
- Each unsafe shot scores 20K immediately.
- Each unsafe hit subtracts 150K from Chaos Bonus, never below 50K.
- Chaos Bonus is displayed persistently in the mode-status area.

## Saucer Bank

Hitting any saucer during unsafe mode:

- Banks the current Chaos Bonus into the uncapped Goblin Bonus.
- Does not reset Chaos Bonus.
- Holds that ball in the saucer.
- Immediately starts the safe phase.

Chaos Bonus can only be banked during unsafe mode.

## Safe Jackpots

- GI is green.
- All six shot areas flash.
- Saucers remain lit.
- The held ball stays for 10 seconds.
- More Time extends the safe phase to 15 seconds.
- The persistent mode-status area shows the live time remaining.
- The safe phase ends when the timer expires or all six areas are collected.
- At the end of the safe phase, the held saucer kickout fires immediately and
  the temporary message reads `MORE CHAOS!`.
- Goblin keeps the hold active until the held saucer switch confirms that the
  ball has physically left. Only then does unsafe play resume.

Each safe area can be collected once per safe phase.

### Immediate safe-shot score

- Base: 100K per shot.
- More Jackpots: 150K per shot.

### Chaos Bonus added by safe shots

Base sequence:

- 100K
- 125K
- 150K
- 175K
- 200K
- 225K

With Bigger Jackpots:

- 150K
- 175K
- 200K
- 225K
- 250K
- 275K

The sequence resets at the beginning of every safe phase. Chaos Bonus remains capped at 1M.

## Safe-shot messages

The persistent countdown stays visible in the mode-status area. Temporary award messages use the separate shared-message area:

1. `SAFE JACKPOT - 100K` or `SAFE JACKPOT - 150K` for one second.
2. `CHAOS BONUS NOW` with the updated value for one second.

## Second saucer during safe mode

If another ball enters a different saucer while one ball is already held:

- Score 50K silently.
- Show `NO NO NO`.
- Eject the second ball after 750 ms.
- Do not bank Chaos Bonus.
- Do not restart or extend the safe timer.
- Once the held ball's saucer switch confirms that the ball has left, the next
  entry into any saucer starts a new full 10/15-second safe-phase hold.

## Case Files

- More Jackpots: safe shots score 150K immediately instead of 100K.
- Bigger Jackpots: safe-shot Chaos Bonus sequence becomes 150K through 275K.
- More Time: safe phase lasts 15 seconds instead of 10.
- Safety Net: start a one-time 10-second ball save after the first proper safe-phase saucer release.
- Shot Assist: consumed on the first physical safe-shot hit; it automatically awards one random remaining safe area using the next value in the sequence.

## Chaos Bonus payout

Every valid unsafe-phase saucer scores the current Chaos Bonus immediately and
adds it to the mode's CHAOS SCORED summary total. Green Goblin no longer creates
a persistent end-of-ball mode bonus.
