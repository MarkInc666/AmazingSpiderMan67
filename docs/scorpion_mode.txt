# Scorpion — Sting Sequence

## Core loop

1. Enter the rooftop. Rooftop entry immediately enables both upper exits.
2. Spin the upper spinner to build the pending Sting Jackpot, then choose either upper exit.
3. The opposite drop bank resets and stages one flashing target.
4. Hit the staged target to collect the jackpot and end the attempt, or let the timer expire.
5. A bank-rubber hit gives one consolation award per attempt but does not end it.
6. Complete three attempts, or four with More Jackpots.

Left and right routes use the same awards. Their challenge comes from the physical shot geometry.

## Scoring

- Base staged-target jackpots: 250K, 500K, 1M, then 1.5M on the fourth More Jackpots attempt.
- Every upper-spinner hit adds 50K to the pending jackpot. Spinner hits do not score separately.
- Rubber partial awards: 200K, 300K, 400K, then 500K.
- A rubber consolation can be collected only once during each attempt. The attempt and timer continue afterward.
- Timeout: no award.
- Bigger Jackpots increases staged target and rubber awards by 50%.

## Timing and helpers

- Base staged-shot window: 8 seconds.
- More Time: 12 seconds.
- More Jackpots: adds a fourth attempt.
- Shot Assist: the first-attempt rubber consolation uses that attempt's full base value instead of its partial value; the attempt still continues.
- Safety Net: starts the standard 10-second case-file ball save.

## Lighting and display

- The staged target insert flashes using the existing Scorpion shows.
- The corresponding left- or right-bank GI lights during the staged attempt.
- Before rooftop entry, status prompts `ENTER ROOFTOP` and shows the pending jackpot.
- On the rooftop, status shows `STING READY` and the spinner-built jackpot value.
- Staged status shows the attempt number and persistent countdown.
- Temporary messages show `SCORPION STING`, `CONSOLATION AWARD`, or `STING MISSED`.

## Completion

After the final attempt, Scorpion scoring stops while the ball remains live. `SCORPION DEFEATED!` displays for two seconds, then the normal villain-complete summary begins. Ball-ending teardown skips this delay.
