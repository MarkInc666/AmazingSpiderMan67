# Scorpion — Sting Sequence

## Core loop

1. Hit the upper spinner twice to fill Venom.
2. Choose either upper exit.
3. The opposite drop bank resets and stages one flashing target.
4. Hit the staged target, hit the bank rubber for a partial award, or let the timer expire.
5. Venom resets after every attempt. Complete three attempts, or four with More Jackpots.

Left and right routes use the same awards. Their challenge comes from the physical shot geometry.

## Scoring

- Upper spinner: 50K additional Scorpion score per spin.
- Two spins qualify each attempt.
- Full staged-target awards: 250K, 500K, 1M, then 1.5M on the fourth More Jackpots attempt.
- Rubber partial awards: 200K, 300K, 400K, then 500K.
- Timeout: no award.
- Bigger Jackpots increases staged target and rubber awards by 50%.

## Timing and helpers

- Base staged-shot window: 8 seconds.
- More Time: 12 seconds.
- More Jackpots: adds a fourth attempt.
- Shot Assist: on the first attempt only, a rubber hit counts as the full target hit.
- Safety Net: starts the standard 10-second case-file ball save.

## Lighting and display

- The staged target insert flashes using the existing Scorpion shows.
- The corresponding left- or right-bank GI lights during the staged attempt.
- Build status shows `VENOM: 0/2`.
- Staged status shows the attempt number and persistent countdown.
- Temporary messages show `SCORPION STING`, `PARTIAL STING`, or `STING MISSED`.

## Completion

After the final attempt, Scorpion scoring stops while the ball remains live. `SCORPION DEFEATED!` displays for two seconds, then the normal villain-complete summary begins. Ball-ending teardown skips this delay.
