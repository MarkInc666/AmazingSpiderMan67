Centaur — Charge the Trap
=========================

Implementation status
---------------------
Implemented in MPF 0.80-compatible Python/YAML. Needs machine playtest for
shot timing, gate travel, post staging, and physical light-color readability.

Approved gameplay
-----------------
- Build phase uses both drop banks. Each unique drop scores 25K and adds 100K
  to the Centaur Jackpot. Bank rubbers score 25K.
- Four unique drops open the rooftop gate.
- The ball must enter the rooftop and take the upper-left exit.
- The post holds for 6 seconds, or releases early through the established
  two-flipper cancel. The right bank resets, then targets 2, 3, and 4 drop.
- The staged finale lasts 6 seconds, or 12 seconds with More Time.
- Right-bank rubber awards the built Jackpot.
- Right target 1 or 5 awards 50K consolation unless Shot Assist is active.
- Each staged finale is one-shot: the first right rubber or outer target hit
  resolves that attempt. The timer may also expire without a result.

More Jackpots
-------------
The former left-rubber half-Jackpot behavior has been replaced.

After the first finale resolves or times out, the rooftop gate opens for a
fixed 12-second re-entry window. Entering the rooftop closes the gate and
prompts the upper-left exit. Taking that exit restages the post and right bank
for one second finale. The second finale is also one-shot only. If the re-entry
window expires, no second finale is staged.

Other Case Files
----------------
- Bigger Jackpots: doubles the built Centaur Jackpot.
- More Time: changes each final-shot timer from 6 seconds to 12 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts the shared 10-second Case File ball save when a finale is
  staged.
- Shot Assist: right target 1 or 5 awards the full Jackpot rather than 50K.

Presentation
------------
- Slow yellow bank-GI pulses identify the four-drop build.
- A green ramp/top-GI pulse directs the player to rooftop access.
- Yellow upper-left/upper-GI pulses identify the required left exit.
- The two outer right inserts flash through their green lenses while the
  right-bank GI pulses red for the one-shot finale.
- Messages explicitly call out the gate, left exit, second-chance countdown,
  staged attempt number, and ONE SHOT ONLY.

Validation boundary
-------------------
Static Python, YAML, duplicate-key, event-coverage, workbook, and ZIP checks
are part of the patch workflow. Physical gate, post, switch, lamp, and timing
behavior still requires machine testing.
