THE MOLEMEN — IMPLEMENTED RULES
================================

Status
------
Implemented. Requires on-machine playtest and balance verification.

Core gameplay
-------------
The Molemen is a two-ball area-control multiball. The rooftop gate remains closed throughout the mode.

Three lower-playfield areas independently light their matching saucer jackpots:

* Left pop bumper -> Saucer 1
* Center web target -> Saucer 2
* Right pop bumper -> Saucer 3

Area scoring
------------
* Left/right pop hit: 50,000
* Center web hit: 100,000
* Bigger Jackpots: pop hits become 75,000 and center-web hits become 150,000

Saucer jackpots
---------------
A lit saucer awards 250,000 multiplied by the number of balls currently in play.

Bigger Jackpots raises the base to 300,000 per ball in play.

After collection, that area's hit count and jackpot state reset so the area can be rebuilt.

Add-a-ball
----------
Each area can qualify add-a-ball once:

* Left pop: 3 hits
* Center web: 1 hit
* Right pop: 3 hits

Shoot the matching lit saucer to collect the jackpot and add a ball. Add-a-ball is limited to once per area.

More Jackpots lowers the left/right pop requirement from 3 hits to 2 hits.

One-ball grace
--------------
When multiball drops to one ball, a short grace timer begins before the mode ends:

* Normal: 5 seconds
* More Time: 10 seconds

If another ball is added during grace, the mode continues.

Case Files
----------
* More Jackpots: left/right add-a-ball qualifies after 2 pop hits instead of 3.
* Bigger Jackpots: area scores increase and saucer jackpot base increases from 250,000 to 300,000 per ball.
* More Time: one-ball grace increases from 5 to 10 seconds.
* Safety Net: opening multiball save increases from 15 to 25 seconds.
* Shot Assist: the left web target also counts as a center-web hit.

Presentation and tracking
-------------------------
The widget and status display track the three areas, lit saucers, add-a-ball readiness, total jackpots, biggest jackpot, opening save, and grace time.

Playtest checklist
------------------
1. Confirm two-ball multiball starts and the rooftop gate remains closed.
2. Confirm each area lights only its mapped saucer.
3. Confirm saucer value scales with balls in play.
4. Confirm each area's add-a-ball can be awarded only once.
5. Confirm saucer lights and area progress reset after collection.
6. Confirm the 5-second one-ball grace and 10-second More Time grace.
7. Confirm all Case File effects and helper text.
8. Confirm saucer ejection delay and mode cleanup at ball end.
