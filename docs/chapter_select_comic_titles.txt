ASM67 — CHAPTER SELECT COMIC TITLES
Updated: August 18, 2026

Presentation rule

- Chapter Select presents each chapter as a newly released collectible comic.
- The visible comic title is the chapter's mini-wizard title, not the former
  roster/category label.
- Internal chapter keys and numbered progression variables remain unchanged for
  save-game and code compatibility.
- AVAILABLE, LOCKED, and COLLECTED remain live Godot labels because their values
  change during play.
