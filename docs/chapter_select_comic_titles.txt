ASM67 — CHAPTER SELECT COMIC TITLES
Updated: August 18, 2026

Presentation rule

- Chapter Select presents each chapter as a newly released collectible comic.
- The visible comic title is the chapter's mini-wizard title, not the former
  roster/category label.
- Internal chapter keys and numbered progression variables remain unchanged for
  save-game and code compatibility.
- AVAILABLE, LOCKED, and COLLECTED remain live Godot labels because their values
  change during play.

Chapter 1 artwork

- `images/chapter_1_cover.png` is the text-free 630x725 comic cover.
- `images/chapter_1_title_overlay.png` is the transparent SINISTER SURGE title.
- Chapter Select conditionally displays both layers when Chapter 1 is in the
  left, center, or right carousel position.
- Generic number/title labels are hidden for Chapter 1 so they do not duplicate
  the title artwork.

Chapter 2 artwork

- `images/chapter_2_cover.png` is the text-free 630x725 Mastermind Trap cover.
- Doctor Octopus is centered in a wide inner frame. Mysterio, Scorpion, Lizard,
  and Parafino appear in four symmetrical corner circles.
- `images/chapter_2_title_overlay.png` supplies the transparent horizontal
  MASTERMIND TRAP title below Doctor Octopus's face.
- Chapter Select conditionally displays both layers in the left, center, or
  right carousel position and suppresses the generic labels for Chapters 1–2.

Chapter 3 artwork

- `images/chapter_3_cover.png` is the finished 630x725 Trubble Unleashed cover.
- It uses the 1967-series artwork for Miss Trubble, Pandora's box, Cerberus,
  Cyclops, Diana, Vulcan, and the Centaur, with the title baked into the cover.
- Chapter Select conditionally displays the cover in the left, center, or right
  carousel position and suppresses the generic labels for Chapters 1–3.
- AVAILABLE, LOCKED, and COLLECTED remain separate live labels off the cover.

Chapter 4 artwork

- `images/chapter_4_cover.png` is the text-free 630x725 Crime Wave cover.
- The original tall C composition is preserved without horizontal stretching.
  A navy, red, and cream comic-book frame uses wide side borders and narrow
  top/bottom borders to fill the Chapter Select cover ratio.
- The citywide crime-map composition includes the Chapter 4 villains and a
  glowing Spider-Man swinging across the skyline.
- `images/chapter_4_title_overlay.png` supplies the separate transparent
  horizontal CRIME WAVE title across the bottom of the cover.
- Chapter Select conditionally displays the cover in the left, center, or right
  carousel position with its title overlay and suppresses the generic labels
  for Chapters 1–4.
- AVAILABLE, LOCKED, and COLLECTED remain separate live labels off the cover.
