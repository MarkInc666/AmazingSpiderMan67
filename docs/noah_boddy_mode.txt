DR. NOAH BODDY — FIND THE INVISIBLE TARGET

STATUS
Implemented. Lifecycle, gate, and shared VUK-chase repairs require physical-machine testing.

CORE GAMEPLAY
- Rooftop access opens at mode start. The shared VUK route chase guides the
  player to the roof and stops when the upper-entrance switch is reached.
- The first rooftop visit secretly selects one of the eight lower drop targets.
- Each upper-target hit eliminates one random false location. Eliminated
  locations persist across rooftop visits and are physically re-dropped after
  both banks reset on the next entry.
- Leaving the rooftop before completing the reveal, or hitting a lower drop
  prematurely, resets both banks but preserves the secret target and eliminated
  locations. The shared route chase restarts until the player returns upstairs.
- The upper spinner adds 25,000 per hit to the uncapped Secret Jackpot and banks
  the effective increase into persistent INVISIBLE FORTUNE.
- When only the secret drop remains, a 16-second hurry-up begins. Hit that drop
  to collect the Secret Jackpot. Expiry displays NOAH BODDY ESCAPED.

SCORING
- Secret Jackpot starts at 250,000.
- Upper-target reveal hits score 25,000.
- Bigger Jackpots doubles the full Secret Jackpot, including spinner additions.
- More Jackpots changes upper-target reveal scoring to 75,000.

CASE FILES
- More Jackpots: upper-target reveals score 75,000.
- Bigger Jackpots: doubles the base and spinner-built Secret Jackpot.
- More Time: extends the hurry-up from 16 to 24 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts its ball save when the secret target is revealed.
- Shot Assist: the rubber from the secret target's bank collects the Jackpot.

PROGRESSION AND PRESENTATION
- State 1 means the mode is currently playing.
- Both the collected Jackpot and an expired/missed objective finish at state 2,
  because completed progression is what matters. Their result messages remain
  distinct: the Jackpot display indicates success; failure displays NOAH BODDY
  ESCAPED / SECRET TARGET LOST.
- Rooftop access is requested once from Python; the duplicate YAML request has
  been removed.
- The shared VUK chase stops on roof entry, success, failure, and mode cleanup.
