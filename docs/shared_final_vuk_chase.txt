SHARED FINAL VUK ROUTE CHASE
Rules sync: August 24, 2026

The always-active light_feedback mode owns a shared copy of the Master Vine VUK
route chase. final_vuk_chase_start loops the five-step, 200ms-per-step route:

1. Lower spinner insert: white.
2. Lower-left and spinner GI: green.
3. Upper-left GI: green.
4. Ramp GI: green.
5. Top VUK GI: green.

final_vuk_chase_stop stops the show and explicitly clears all six route lights,
including l_gi_spinner, so no green step can remain behind.

The shared chase is enabled only while the VUK is the required final objective
in Conner's Reptiles, Spider-Slayer, Super Swami's optional Blackout phase, The
Plotter's VUK phase, Professor Pretorius, Dr. Von Schlick, and Kotep. It is also
enabled while a chapter mini-wizard is ready at the Daily Bugle VUK. It is not
used for the final wizard ready flag because Final Showdown starts from a lower
saucer rather than the VUK.

All participating modes post the stop event on collection, timeout/failure, and
mode cleanup. The chapter mini-wizard path stops it as soon as the VUK starts the
wizard intro.

Final Showdown qualification handoff (August 31, 2026): after Comic #4 is
collected at the end of a chapter wizard summary, the existing controlled
chapter-transition ball save releases the drained ball to the mechanical shooter
lane instead of opening Chapter Select. The display shows:

  KINGPIN ENCOUNTER
  SHOOT ANY SAUCER TO START

The reminder repeats while Final Showdown is ready. All three lower saucer
clusters flash together until any saucer is hit; that saucer starts the normal
Final Showdown / Kingpin bookend intro. Starting the final wizard clears the
special saucer show and cancels the reminder.
