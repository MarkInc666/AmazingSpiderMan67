THE SPIDER-MEN — ALIGN THE HOMEWORLD RAY

STATUS

Coded. Needs on-machine playtest.


CORE RULES

The mode uses the same six switches as the normal skill shot:

- Saucer 1
- Saucer 2
- Saucer 3
- Star rollover
- A rollover
- B rollover

All six positions begin pulsing red. The three saucers use their paired red
hole lamps; the green numbered 1/2/3 inserts are not used. Star, A and B use
their normal inserts and are also driven red.

Left and right flipper presses rotate the entire six-position light pattern.
Completed and incomplete states move together. Hitting a pulsing position
converts it to solid red. Hitting a solid position scores a Fine Adjustment but
does not advance the alignment.

The first Jackpot starts the proton countdown. Complete all six positions before
the countdown expires to align the Homeworld Ray and send the Spider-Men home.
The rooftop gate is closed for the entire mode because all six shots are on the
main playfield.


TIMER

- Base countdown: 40 seconds
- More Time countdown: 60 seconds
- The timer starts only when the first pulsing position is completed.
- Later Jackpots do not reset or extend it.
- Timer expiry fails the mode.


SCORING

- New pulsing position: 100,000 Jackpot
- New pulsing position with Bigger Jackpots: 150,000 Jackpot
- Sixth position: double the current Jackpot value (200,000 or 300,000)
- Already-solid position: 25,000 Fine Adjustment
- More Jackpots center-web Super: 500,000

Shot Assist's free position awards its normal 100,000 or 150,000 and counts as
a Jackpot. The Super is included in Mode Points but not in the regular Jackpot
count.


CASE FILES

- More Jackpots: after all six positions are solid, flash the center web for a
  fixed 500,000 Super Jackpot for 10 seconds. Collecting it or letting the
  window expire completes the mode.
- Bigger Jackpots: increase alignment Jackpots from 100,000 to 150,000; the
  sixth remains double. The fixed Super remains 500,000.
- More Time: increase the proton countdown from 40 to 60 seconds.
- Safety Net: start the standard 10-second Case File ball save.
- Shot Assist: the first completed pulsing position also completes one random
  uncompleted position and awards its normal value.


LIGHTING

Alignment lights pulse/hold red. The full playfield GI uses the shared physical
height bands 1-8 as a continuous bottom-to-top chase. All bands are blue while
one band is red; the red band advances from band 1 through band 8 every 250ms
and then repeats.

During the optional Super window, the six positions remain solid red, the
blue/red band chase continues, and the center web flashes red.


SUMMARY

- Mode Points
- Jackpots
- Fine Adjustments
