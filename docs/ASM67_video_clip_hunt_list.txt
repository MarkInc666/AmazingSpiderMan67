ASM67 — VIDEO CLIP HUNT LIST
Spider-Man (1967–1970)
Purpose: Find a strong 1–2 minute clip for every villain/wizard mode still needing a dedicated video.

NOTES
- Prefer a clip with the villain clearly visible, recognizable action, and minimal dead dialogue/setup.
- For wizard modes, there is usually no single matching TV episode. Use the listed chapter episode pool to build/select a clip that represents the wizard's combined villains/theme.
- Episode numbering can vary between sources because Season 1 and Season 3 often split a half-hour into A/B segments. Use the TITLE as the primary search key.
- "Primary" means the first place I would look for a usable 1–2 minute clip.

VILLAINS STILL NEEDING DEDICATED VIDEOS
======================================================================

[ ] DR. NOAH BODDY
Primary: "Spider-Man Meets Doctor Noah Boddy" — Season 1, 12A
Alternate: "To Catch a Spider" — Season 1, 19A
Look for: invisible robberies, Jameson/printing-press attack, invisible-object movement, reveal/capture.
Best bet: the printing-press confrontation gives you Boddy + Spider-Man action in one sequence.

[ ] DR. MAGNETO
Primary: "The Revenge of Dr. Magneto" — Season 1, 10A
Look for: magnetic sabotage, moving metal, train/monument/building sequences, anti-magnetic web defeat.

[ ] PROFESSOR PRETORIUS
Primary: "The Birth of Micro-Man" — Season 3, 44B
Look for: Pretorius and the Kingdom Come Machine, Spider-Man shrinking, miniature lab/cat/monkey sequences.
Note: episode sources commonly spell the character Pretorius.

[ ] DOCTOR DUMPTY
Primary: "The Devious Dr. Dumpty" — Season 3, 45B
Look for: balloon devices, laughing gas, parade/heist action, Dumpty escape/capture.

[ ] DR. VON SCHLICK
Primary: "The Slippery Doctor Von Schlick" — Season 1, 15B
Look for: oil theft, slippery/oil attacks, barge/sewer chase, final capture.

[ ] CLIVE AND BLOTTO
Primary: "Blotto" — Season 2, Episode 15
Look for: Clive operating the Spirit-Scope, Blotto consuming cars/buildings, Spirit-Scope reversal.
Best bet: Blotto rampaging through the city or the final Spirit-Scope confrontation.

[ ] DOCTOR ZAPP
Primary: "Spider-Man Meets Skyboy" — Season 2, Episode 17
Look for: Dr. Zapp at Lightning Mountain, electrical attacks, Astro-Helmet plot, Spider-Man/Skyboy rescue.

[ ] BOLTON AND BOOMER
Primary: "Thunder Rumble" — Season 2, Episode 16
Look for: Bolton/Boomer, lightning attacks, Martian competition, Spider-Man vs. Boomer.
Note: older project material used "Voltan"; the episode/cast references identify Bolton and Boomer.

[ ] THE SNOWMAN
Primary: "Trouble with Snow" — Season 3, 41A
Look for: Snowman coming to life, growth/rampage, electrical/copper-cable finale, melting defeat.

[ ] THE PLUTONIANS
Primary: "Sub-Zero for Spidey" — Season 1, 1B
Look for: New York freezing, giant ice beings, Dr. Smartyr kidnapping, ice/freezing visuals.

[ ] DR. MANTA
Primary: "Phantom from the Depths of Time" — Season 2, Episode 8 (overall episode 28)
Look for: Dr. Manta playing the organ, robot beetles, island/lab scenes, enslaved inhabitants.
Important: ignore the old project-guide entry that labeled episode 28 "Sir Galahad."

[ ] IGOR
Primary: "Phantom from the Depths of Time" — Season 2, Episode 8 (overall episode 28)
Look for: Igor with Dr. Manta, island/lab/henchman material, beetle machinery.
Note: Igor shares the Dr. Manta episode, so choose a section where Igor is actually visible rather than generic Manta footage.

[ ] DOCTOR ATLANTEAN
Primary: "Up from Nowhere" — Season 3, Episode 7 / overall 46
Look for: Manhattan under the dome, Atlanteans, submarine/underwater assault, city sinking/restoration.

[ ] DEVARGAS
Primary: "Cloud City of Gold" — Season 2, Episode 12 / overall 32
Look for: DeVargas in the golden city, giant golden eagle, giant spider, cannon/volcano eruption.
Best bet: DeVargas directing the eagle/cannon or the eruption escape.

[ ] THE MOLEMEN
Primary: "Spider-Man Battles the Molemen" — Season 2, Episode 7 / overall 27
Alternate: "Menace from the Bottom of the World" — Season 2, Episode 5 / overall 25
Look for: underground city, Molemen attacks, stolen/sinking buildings, Mugs Riley/Molemen confrontations.

[ ] CHARLES CAMEO
Primary: "Double Identity" — Season 1, 19B
Alternate: "The Sinister Prime Minister" — Season 1, 10B
Look for: Cameo impersonating Peter/Spider-Man, disguise reveals, the famous two-Spider-Man confrontation.
Best bet: "Double Identity" because it gives you the fake Spider-Man visual immediately.

[ ] BRUTUS
Primary: "Double Identity" — Season 1, 19B
Look for: Brutus protecting Cameo, knocking down Spider-Man, attacking/replacing the police officer, physical fight scenes.
Note: this is Cameo's human bodyguard, not the unrelated dog also named Brutus.

[ ] DESPERADO
Primary: "Spider-Man vs. Desperado" — Season 3, 41B
Look for: electronic horse, lasso/gun attacks, bank robberies, Spider-Man pursuit/capture.

[ ] SKYMASTER
Primary: "Criminals in the Clouds" — Season 2, Episode 4 / overall 24
Look for: hidden airship, Roy Robinson kidnapping, flying-base infiltration, airship crash.

[ ] CONNER'S REPTILES
Primary: "Conner's Reptiles" — Season 3, 40B
Look for: intelligent reptile/alligator, Connors held captive, reptile army, Spanish-fort action, serum defeat.

[ ] SIR GALAHAD
Primary: "Knight Must Fall" — Season 3, 45A
Look for: motorcycle-riding knight, electrified lance, jousting/fight footage.
Important: this is the real Sir Galahad episode; do not confuse it with old episode-number labels in the project guide.

[ ] MASTER VINE
Primary: "Vine" — Season 2, Episode 10 / overall 30
Alternate: "Rollarama" — Season 3, Episode 8 / overall 47 for additional dimensional/plant-like imagery if useful
Look for: prehistoric plant kingdom, Master Vine, arena/creatures, radium gems, giant vines.

[ ] MASTER TECHNICIAN
Primary: "Swing City" — Season 2, Episode 3 / overall 23
Alternate: "Specialists and Slaves" — Season 3, Episode 11 / overall 50
Look for: nuclear-reactor takeover, Manhattan levitated into the air, radiation machinery, villain control room.
Note: he returns later under the Radiation Specialist identity.

[ ] THE SPIDER-MEN
Primary: "Home" — Season 2, Episode 14 / overall 34
Look for: Carol using spider powers, alien Spider-Men, underground alien city, proton device/homeworld ray material.
Best bet: Spider-Man following Carol and meeting the alien Spider-Men.

[ ] BARON VON RANTENRAVEN
Primary: "Sky Harbor" — Season 3, 42A
Look for: Baron, sky pirates, biplanes, zeppelins, Sky Harbor fortress, fortress crash.
Best bet: rooftop/airship battle footage should match the pinball mode especially well.
