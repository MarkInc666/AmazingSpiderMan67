FIDDLER MODE

STATUS
Implemented. The startup lockout and three-mistake rule require physical-machine playtesting.

CORE GAMEPLAY
- Watch the demonstrated note pattern and repeat it in order.
- Round 1 contains one note, Round 2 contains two notes, and Round 3 contains three notes.
- More Jackpots adds a four-note encore round.
- Each required light demonstrates its position with one, two, three, or four short pulses, repeated four times, then remains solid.
- Correct-note values are 100K; 200K/300K; 400K/500K/600K; and 700K/800K/900K/1M for the optional fourth round.

WRONG NOTES
- Fiddler allows three mistakes during the entire mode.
- The first and second mistakes play violin_bad_note, reset the current round to its first note, and replay the complete pattern after 1.5 seconds.
- The third mistake ends the mode and Fiddler escapes.
- Mistakes do not erase points already scored in earlier rounds or earlier correct notes.
- Shot Assist forgives the first wrong input, awards the expected note, and does not consume a mistake.

INPUT LOCKOUT
- Note inputs are ignored from mode start until the first demonstration is complete.
- Inputs remain locked while drop targets are being knocked down, during round setup delays, throughout demonstrations, and during wrong-note replay delays.
- Inputs become live only when PLAY THE PATTERN is shown.

CASE FILES
- More Jackpots: adds the four-note encore round.
- Bigger Jackpots: adds 100K to every correct note.
- More Time: repeats an unfinished pattern after 8 seconds instead of 14 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts a 10-second ball save.
- Shot Assist: forgives the first wrong input and awards the expected note.

SUMMARY
- Completing all required rounds reports FIDDLER SILENCED.
- Reaching three mistakes reports FIDDLER ESCAPED.
- Summary stats remain ROUND and NOTES HIT.
