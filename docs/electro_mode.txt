⚡ Electro
Mode: “Power Surge”

Core flow
- A spark travels randomly among eight shot groups:
  left web, main spinner, left drops, saucers, center web,
  upper spinner, upper targets, and right drops.
- Lower-playfield spark windows last 5 seconds; upper spinner and upper
  target spark windows last 10 seconds.
- If the lit shot is missed, it remains available and the spark moves.
- Every new or moved spark resets to its maximum jackpot value. Lower
  sparks then decay once per second; upper sparks decay once every 2 seconds.
- Collected shot groups are disabled until one final group remains.
- The final group becomes a Super Jackpot: 10 seconds for a lower shot
  or 15 seconds for the upper spinner and upper targets.

Scoring
- Normal spark starts at 250K and falls by 10K per decay step to a 50K floor.
- Bigger Jackpots starts normal sparks at 300K and raises the Super
  Jackpot from 1M to 1.25M.
- More Time doubles the decay intervals: lower sparks decay every 2 seconds
  and upper sparks decay every 4 seconds.
- More Jackpots returns the first collected group to the pool, allowing
  one additional normal spark jackpot before the Super.
- Shot Assist makes the first lower spark last 8 seconds or the first
  upper spark last 13 seconds.

Presentation and devices
- Existing Electro light-show timing is retained.
- The Super uses a 10-second lower or 15-second upper countdown and
  persistent SUPER SPARK status showing the required shot.
- The rooftop gate opens only for upper-playfield spark shots.
- Ordinary and unlit saucer hits use delayed clearing. If the saucers
  collect the final Super, the ball remains held through the villain
  summary and is kicked out when the summary finishes.
