⚡ Electro
Mode: “Power Surge”

Core flow
- A spark travels randomly among eight shot groups:
  left web, main spinner, left drops, saucers, center web,
  upper spinner, upper targets, and right drops.
- Normal spark windows last 5 seconds.
- If the lit shot is missed, it remains available and the spark moves.
- Every new or moved spark resets to its maximum jackpot value before
  the once-per-second value decay starts again.
- Collected shot groups are disabled until one final group remains.
- The final group becomes a 10-second Super Jackpot.

Scoring
- Normal spark starts at 250K and falls 10K per second to a 50K floor.
- Bigger Jackpots starts normal sparks at 300K and raises the Super
  Jackpot from 1M to 1.25M.
- More Time halves the normal jackpot decay rate.
- More Jackpots returns the first collected group to the pool, allowing
  one additional normal spark jackpot before the Super.
- Shot Assist makes the first normal spark window last 8 seconds.

Presentation and devices
- Existing Electro light-show timing is retained.
- The Super uses the SUPER SURGE LIT 10-second countdown and persistent
  SUPER SPARK status showing the required shot.
- The rooftop gate opens only for upper-playfield spark shots.
- Saucer groups use delayed clearing.
