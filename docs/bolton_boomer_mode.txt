BOLTON AND BOOMER — FINAL MODE DESIGN
=====================================

Core loop
---------
- Start a two-ball multiball. MPF owns ball addition and multiball ending.
- Opening ball save is 15 seconds, or 25 seconds with Safety Net.
- One random playfield target pulses yellow.
- Hit the lit target for 100K, or 150K with Bigger Jackpots.
- All three saucers then pulse red and can capture the first ball.
- Capturing one ball scores 100K, or 150K with Bigger Jackpots.
- The captured saucer stays solid red, the rooftop gate opens, and the VUK flashes green.
- If the second ball enters any saucer, it is kicked out after one second; the original held ball remains.
- Shoot the VUK with the remaining ball to collect the Super Jackpot.

Super sequence
--------------
- First Super is untimed.
- Second and later Supers have 20 seconds, or 30 seconds with More Time.
- Super starts at 500K, or 750K with Bigger Jackpots.
- More Jackpots adds 100K to each next collected Super, capped at 1.5M.
- On collect: gate closes immediately, VUK kicks after two seconds, and the held saucer kicks two seconds later.
- A new random target lights after both balls are released.
- On timed miss: gate closes immediately, held saucer releases after two seconds, and the target stage restarts.
- Mode ends when multiball ends.

Case Files
----------
- More Jackpots: each next Super increases by 100K, capped at 1.5M.
- Bigger Jackpots: target 150K, capture 150K, base Super 750K.
- More Time: timed Supers last 30 seconds.
- Safety Net: opening multiball save extends from 15 to 25 seconds.
- Shot Assist: on the first timed Super, reaching 10 seconds automatically awards the Super.

Presentation
------------
- Lit random target: pulsing yellow.
- Saucer capture ready: all saucers pulse red.
- Held saucer: solid red.
- VUK Super ready: flashing green.
- Timed status: SUPER IN X.
