THE PLOTTER — CURRENT MODE RULES

STATUS
- Updated and coded.
- Automated validation completed with the patch.
- Physical-machine playtest still required.


CORE LOOP
- Pop hits build Rumors and score 25,000.
- Two Rumors make the lower playfield spinner ready.
- The lower spinner consumes two Rumors, scores 50,000, and lights one random
  saucer.
- The upper spinner is not used by this mode.
- A lit saucer scores a 250,000 Scheme Jackpot. Stop three schemes to expose
  The Plotter.
- After the third scheme, surplus lit saucers are cleared, the rooftop gate
  opens, and the Daily Bugle VUK lights for 20 seconds.
- The VUK Super scores 1,000,000 and completes the normal mode.
- Missing the VUK before the timer expires ends the attempt without completing
  The Plotter for progression.


CASE FILES
- More Jackpots: after the 1,000,000 VUK Super, all three upper targets flash
  together. Any one scores a 500,000 Back Page Bonus before the original VUK
  timer expires. The VUK Super and Back Page values are not increased by
  Bigger Jackpots.
- Bigger Jackpots: doubles ordinary Plotter scoring only:
  - Pops: 25,000 becomes 50,000.
  - Spinner: 50,000 becomes 100,000.
  - Lit saucer Scheme Jackpot: 250,000 becomes 500,000.
  - VUK Super remains 1,000,000.
  - Back Page Bonus remains 500,000.
- More Time: extends the VUK/Back Page timer from 20 seconds to 30 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts a 10-second ball save when the mode begins.
- Shot Assist: the first unlit saucer hit while at least one saucer is lit
  collects one randomly selected lit saucer. Correctly hitting a lit saucer
  does not consume the assist. A saucer hit when none are lit does not consume
  it. After the assisted collection, later saucers must match a lit saucer.


LIGHTING
- Base GI remains amber throughout the mode.
- With fewer than two Rumors available, both pop-bumper lamp rings pulse green.
- With two or more Rumors and an available saucer, the lower spinner insert
  flashes yellow. The upper spinner is never lit for Plotter.
- Each lit saucer flashes its two physical red side inserts. Multiple lit
  saucers can flash at the same time.
- If all three saucers are lit, pop and spinner guidance turns off so only the
  three red saucer objectives remain.
- When the VUK phase begins, all pop, spinner-objective, and saucer lighting is
  cleared. The lower spinner insert and the adjacent upper-left VUK GI pulse
  yellow for the entire VUK timer.
- After the VUK Super with More Jackpots, the VUK guidance stops and all three
  white-lens upper-target inserts flash red together until collected or expired.
- All Plotter shows stop on completion, failure, ball end, or mode stop, and GI
  returns to the normal white state.


MESSAGES AND STATUS
- Start: POPS BUILD RUMORS.
- Second available Rumor: SPINNER READY / SHOOT THE YELLOW LOWER SPINNER.
- Spinner attempted too early: NEED RUMORS / HIT POPS.
- Spinner collection: SAUCER LIT / COLLECT THE RED SAUCER.
- Lit saucer: SCHEME STOPPED plus progress toward three.
- Assisted unlit saucer: SHOT ASSIST plus the lit saucer collected.
- VUK phase: THE PLOTTER EXPOSED / SHOOT DAILY BUGLE VUK plus time remaining.
- More Jackpots: THE PLOTTER SUPER / BACK PAGE LIT plus time remaining.
- Timer expiry distinguishes a missed VUK from a missed Back Page.


PLAYER VARIABLE OWNERSHIP
- plotter_state is retained for persistent progression.
- active_mode_points, active_mode_stat_1, and active_mode_stat_2 carry the
  standard shared summary values. Hits count total Rumors earned rather than
  the unspent Rumors remaining when the mode ends.
- Rumors, lit saucers, schemes, VUK/Back Page state, timer state, lighting phase,
  and Shot Assist use remain in Python rather than Plotter-specific player vars.
