DEVARGAS — CITY OF GOLD

STATUS
Implemented; needs machine playtest.

CORE RULE
DeVargas is a single-ball pure score attack. Collect as much gold as possible
from 20 timed opportunities. More Jackpots increases the total to 25. The mode
always completes successfully when every opportunity resolves or when the ball
drains; all previously collected gold remains banked.

SHOT POOL
Each opportunity is selected randomly from 13 repeatable groups:

1. Left web target
2. Center web target
3. Left pop
4. Right pop
5. Left 3-bank
6. Right 5-bank
7. A rollover
8. B rollover
9. Middle A rollover
10. Middle B rollover
11. Upper targets (all three form one group)
12. Main spinner
13. Upper spinner

A shot may repeat after another group has been selected, but not as the
immediately following opportunity. A currently active group cannot be selected
again. For either drop-bank group, any target or the rear rubber collects the
gold. Banks are not reset when selected; they reset normally when completed.

TIMING AND OVERLAP
Base opportunity timing:

- Slow pulse: 4 seconds
- Medium pulse: 3 seconds
- Fast pulse: 3 seconds
- Total availability: 10 seconds

A new opportunity appears every 8 seconds. This produces a natural 2-second
overlap without an artificial active-shot limit. Hitting an active shot removes
that opportunity, immediately selects a replacement, and restarts the 8-second
selection interval.

More Time changes the timing to 5 seconds slow, 4 seconds medium, and 4 seconds
fast. The selection interval remains 8 seconds, producing a 5-second overlap.

SCORING AND BONUS BANK
Base values:

- Slow: 150,000
- Medium: 125,000
- Fast: 100,000

Bigger Jackpots values:

- Slow: 200,000
- Medium: 175,000
- Fast: 150,000

Each collected value is added immediately to the player's score and in full to
the persistent devargas_bonus bank. The end-of-ball bonus displays this bank as
DEVARGAS GOLD.

CASE FILES
- More Jackpots: 25 opportunities instead of 20.
- Bigger Jackpots: values become 200K / 175K / 150K.
- More Time: opportunity phases become 5s / 4s / 4s.
- Safety Net: standard 10-second opening ball save.
- Shot Assist: the first expired opportunity auto-collects once at the fast-tier
  value, including the Bigger Jackpots value when active.

LIGHTING
The mode background GI is light blue 8080D0. Active shot indicators pulse from
gold F0F060 to dim gold 303000. Slow, medium, and fast phases run at one, two,
and four full pulses per second respectively. When an opportunity resolves, its
indicator returns to the light-blue GI background or its normal insert state.

The rooftop gate opens only while the upper-target group or upper spinner is
active. It closes when neither remains active. The A/B rollovers and middle A/B
rollovers are lower-playfield groups and never control the gate.

DAILY BUGLE AND DEVICES
Daily Bugle Mystery is disabled during the mode because both A/B rollover pairs
are owned by DeVargas. A VUK hit ejects after 1.5 seconds. Saucers are neutral
and eject after 750 ms. Daily Bugle state is restored when the mode stops.

MESSAGES
- Start: CITY OF GOLD / HIT THE PULSING SHOTS
- Collection: GOLD COLLECTED / timing tier / value
- Expiration: GOLD LOST / shot name
- Shot Assist: GOLD RECOVERED / SHOT ASSIST / value
- Status: gold banked this mode, opportunities used, and gold shots collected
