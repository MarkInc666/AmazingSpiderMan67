PARDO - HYPNOSIS REEL
=====================

OBJECTIVE
---------
- The player has five chances to defeat Pardo.
- Each chance presents three lit shots selected from seven possible shot groups.
- The spinner reveals which of the three shots is correct.
- Three correct shots defeats Pardo immediately.
- If all available chances are used with fewer than three correct shots, Pardo escapes.

REVEAL
------
- A spinner hit reveals the correct shot for 4 seconds.
- All three hidden choices are solid green so the white and green physical lenses
  display a consistent color.
- During the reveal, the correct shot flashes green and the two wrong choices turn off.
- When the reveal expires, all three choices return to their hidden appearance.
- A correct or wrong choice briefly flashes one GI section green or red, then restores
  that section to Pardo's normal GI color after 350ms.

SCORING
-------
- Correct shots score a Hypnosis Jackpot.
- The base Jackpot starts at 100K and increases by 50K after each correct shot.
- Wrong choices score 10K and consume the current chance.
- The summary reports correct shots, wrong shots, and total mode points.

CASE FILES
----------
- More JP: seven chances instead of five.
- Bigger JP: Jackpots start at 150K and increase by 75K.
- More Time: the spinner reveal lasts 6 seconds instead of 4 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts the standard 10-second case-file ball save.
- Shot Assist: all three choices are treated as correct during the first chance.

SHOTS
-----
- Left web
- Center web
- Upper-target group (left, center, and right upper targets always share one state)
- Left drop bank
- Right drop bank
- Left pop
- Right pop

DEVICE BEHAVIOR
---------------
- Rooftop access opens whenever the upper-target group is one of the current choices.
- The Daily Bugle VUK uses the shared regular release delay.
- Saucers are not choices and use the shared saucer kickout action.
