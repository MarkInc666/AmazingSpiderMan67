HARLEY CLIVENDON — HYPNOTIC HOLD

Two-ball multiball. Lock one ball in any saucer for 50K, 
then use the other ball to light eight lower-playfield areas: 
left/right sling, left/right pop, left/right bank, left web, and center web. 
Any single bank target or matching bank rubber qualifies that bank area; normal bank resets remain active.

Each newly lit area scores 50K. At four lit areas the Daily Bugle VUK lights. 
The player may collect immediately or continue building. 
The VUK jackpot is 100K per lit area, or 150K with Bigger Jackpots. 
On collect, the VUK and held saucer ball eject and a new round begins. 
More Jackpots preserves two random lit areas. 
The mode ends when multiball returns to one ball.

Helpers: More Time changes the opening multiball save from 15s to 25s; Safety Net starts a one-time ball save after the first VUK jackpot; Shot Assist spots one random unlit area after the first area hit without awarding its 50K.

Summary: MODE SCORE, VUK JACKPOTS, BIGGEST JP.
