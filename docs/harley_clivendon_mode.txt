HARLEY CLIVENDON MODE
====================

Implementation status
---------------------
Implemented for MPF 0.80. Physical machine testing remains required for the
multiball autoplunge, saucer releases, gate passage, and occupied-saucer
congestion behavior.

Core rules
----------
- Start controlled two-ball multiball with a 15-second opening ball save.
- The shared multiball autoplunge flag triggers the machine's delayed 2-second
  plunger autofire for Ball 2.
- Capture one ball in any lower saucer for 50K.
- While that ball is held, light eight independent playfield areas.
- Each unique area scores 50K and turns solid blue.
- Four lit areas qualify the VUK Jackpot; additional areas increase its value.
- The VUK Jackpot is repeatable and ends only when multiball reaches one ball.

Areas and lighting
------------------
- Left sling: l_gi_left_sling_upper and l_gi_left_sling_lower act together.
- Right sling: right-sling GI.
- Left and right pop bumpers.
- Left and right drop-bank areas.
- Left and center web targets.
- Available areas pulse grey only while a ball is held.
- Collected areas are solid blue.
- Before a lock, all area guidance stays off.
- Lock availability uses only the six red saucer hole lamps. Green l_1, l_2,
  and l_3 inserts are not used.
- The main spinner insert flashes yellow when the VUK Jackpot is ready.

Jackpot and gate flow
---------------------
- Jackpot value is 100K per lit area, or 150K with Bigger Jackpots.
- Every VUK award also adds its full value to persistent HARLEY JACKPOTS.
- The gate is closed while seeking the saucer lock.
- With a held ball and at least four lit areas, the gate opens for the VUK route.
- It stays open through the delayed VUK kick, then closes at the upper-entrance
  opto or the 5-second fallback.
- The held saucer ball is released exactly once through Harley's explicit
  ownership helper. Broad stop-time saucer cleanup does not issue a second eject.
- The next controlled lock is enabled 2.2 seconds after the Jackpot release.

Round lighting cleanup
----------------------
At VUK collection, every available and collected-area show stops. With More
Jackpots, two areas remain in the internal set but stay dark before the next
lock. When the next ball is captured, all available guidance starts and those
two retained areas are redrawn solid blue. Releasing a held saucer explicitly
stops all held-ball shows.

Case Files
----------
- More Jackpots: retain two areas for the next round.
- Bigger Jackpots: 150K per lit area.
- More Time: 25-second opening save.
- Safety Net: shared ball save after the first VUK Jackpot.
- Shot Assist: first scored area spots another unlit area.

Preserved behavior and test boundary
------------------------------------
Scoring, Case Files, gate timing, VUK timing, persistent HARLEY JACKPOTS,
spinner-ready prompt, and multiball-ending rules are unchanged. The patch does
not add ball-search or automatic congestion rescue behavior. A second ball may
still physically lodge behind the controlled held ball; this remains a machine-
test issue requiring a separately approved strategy.
