ASM67 END-OF-BALL BONUS

The upper score panel continues to show the current player's overall score
throughout the bonus count. Its title uses the current player number and reads
PLAYER 1 SCORE, PLAYER 2 SCORE, PLAYER 3 SCORE, or PLAYER 4 SCORE.

The lower bonus panel counts regular bonus buckets, mode bonuses, Held Bonus,
and Hold Bonus messages. At the end of the sequence, the lower panel displays:

TOTAL BONUS
<total awarded during this bonus count>

The final total includes regular bonus after its multiplier, all mode-bonus
entries awarded on this ball, a carried Held Bonus collected on this ball, and
the duplicated bonus awarded when Hold Bonus is earned on the last ball.

When Hold Bonus is earned before the last ball, the current total is banked for
the next ball but is not added a second time to the current final total.

The final total remains on screen for the existing 2.5-second hold.
Machine testing remains required.
