Chapter select: plunger settling

Extract into the project root and overwrite the included files. Restart MPF.
Apply alongside the previous skill-shot and lane-carryover patch.

Manual plunge selection now requires one continuous second on s_plunger,
followed by 100 milliseconds continuously off the switch. A landing bounce
before the settling time cannot confirm selection. A return to the switch
cancels a pending confirmation and starts the settling period again.

A ball already on the switch when chapter select starts must also settle.
An empty plunger lane cannot confirm from an inactive event alone. Arming
does not select automatically. Flipper navigation and both-flipper selection
remain available immediately. All pending plunger timers are removed on stop.
Timing constants in chapter_select.py: PLUNGER_SETTLE_MS and PLUNGER_RELEASE_MS.

Validation: actual chapter-selection methods passed stubbed scheduler checks
for repeated landing bounce, 999/1000ms settling, 99/100ms release, release
bounce, parked/empty startup, flipper selection and stop cancellation.
Python/YAML parsed. Physical-machine testing remains unconfirmed.
