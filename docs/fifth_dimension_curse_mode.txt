FIFTH DIMENSION CURSE MINI-WIZARD

STATUS
Implemented. The VUK timing correction requires physical-machine playtesting.

VUK RELEASE BEHAVIOR
- During active gameplay, every Daily Bugle VUK visit uses the shared delayed-release service.
- Both an unlit VUK visit and a Curse Jackpot wait 1.5 seconds before the up-kicker fires.
- The shared service checks the VUK switch after the first pulse and may issue one recovery pulse if the ball remains detected.
- The ball captured to start the mini-wizard remains held throughout the introduction.
- The post-introduction release remains immediate; the new 1.5-second gameplay delay does not add another wait after the intro.

GAMEPLAY SUMMARY
- Fifth Dimension Curse is a three-ball multiball.
- Playfield switches illuminate six temporary city zones.
- Zones remain bright for 5 seconds, flicker, and return to dim after 8 seconds.
- Entering the VUK with active zones collects a Curse Jackpot based on the number of active zones plus the chapter Case File bonus.
- Entering the VUK with no active zones scores no Curse Jackpot and uses the same 1.5-second release.
- A VUK Jackpot with at least three active zones qualifies add-a-ball.
- Completing all three upper targets collects a qualified add-a-ball, up to three times.
- The mini-wizard ends when multiball ends.
