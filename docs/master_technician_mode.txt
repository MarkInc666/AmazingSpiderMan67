MASTER TECHNICIAN — CURRENT MODE RULES

STATUS
- Redesigned and coded.
- Automated validation completed with the patch.
- Physical-machine playtest still required.

CORE LOOP
- Master Technician is a one-ball, timed spinner/drop-target score attack.
- The mode starts with 60 seconds and both drop banks reset.
- Each physical or inlane-assisted drop scores 25,000.
- Either inlane knocks down one random standing target.
- The spinner scores 10,000 when no targets are down.
- With one through seven targets down, the spinner scores 50,000 per down target:
  1 = 50,000; 2 = 100,000; 3 = 150,000; 4 = 200,000;
  5 = 250,000; 6 = 300,000; 7 = 350,000.
- Spinner and drop-bank GI pulses increase through seven speeds as the value builds.
- The player should leave one target standing and cash in the spinner value.
- The built spinner value remains active and may be collected repeatedly. Reaching
  seven down takes a substantial portion of the 60-second mode, so a spinner hit
  does not reset either bank or reduce the value.
- Knocking down all eight targets causes a short circuit, deducts 10 seconds,
  resets both banks, and returns the spinner to its unlit 10,000 value.
- The timer penalty is clamped at zero.
- Timer expiration ends the mode normally and counts as completion for progression.
- There is no defeat objective; the goal is to score as much as possible before time expires.
- A ball drain stops the mode normally through the standard villain progression flow.

CASE FILES
- More Jackpots: both web targets light whenever one through seven targets are
  down. Each lit web-target hit scores 50,000. They turn off at zero targets and
  during every eight-target reset.
- Bigger Jackpots: the spinner builds at 75,000 per down target, up to 525,000
  with seven down. The unlit spinner remains 10,000.
- More Time: the starting timer is 75 seconds instead of 60.
- Safety Net: the first eight-target short circuit still resets both banks but
  does not deduct 10 seconds.
- Shot Assist: the first inlane can knock down two random standing targets in
  sequence. It is capped at seven down and will not automatically knock down
  the eighth target.

DISPLAY AND LIGHTING
- Intro cues explain that drops boost the spinner, seven down is the scoring
  goal, and all eight costs 10 seconds.
- Mode status shows seconds remaining, targets down, and current spinner value.
- The Master Technician widget uses the existing Master Technician portrait
  instead of requiring a separate master_technician_1.ogv video file.
- Spinner awards, 50,000 web jackpots, short circuits, Safety Net use, and the
  Shot Assist final-target hold each produce player-facing messages.
- The spinner insert and both bank GI groups pulse faster at each level from one
  through seven targets down.
- Mode GI uses a cool blue base. At zero down, the spinner and both bank GI
  groups are explicitly off. Each drop gives a brief pale-blue bank flash, and
  each spinner award gives a brief yellow/white spinner flash before the current
  level pulse resumes.
- An eight-target short circuit flashes the bank GI red. Safety Net cancellation
  flashes it green. Normal E0E0E0 GI is restored when the mode stops.
- More Jackpots lights both white-lens web-target inserts white.

DEVICE BEHAVIOR
- The rooftop gate closes when the mode starts and remains out of the lower-playfield
  spinner/drop-target objective.
- The unrelated upper-playfield post is not raised at mode start.
- Left and right drop-bank reset coils are staggered by 300ms to avoid firing both
  high-current reset coils simultaneously.

SUMMARY
- Normal title: TIME EXPIRED.
- Ball-drain title: CIRCUIT INTERRUPTED.
- Statistics: spinner hits, short circuits, and total mode points.

PLAYER VARIABLE OWNERSHIP
- master_technician_state is retained for persistent progression.
- active_mode_points, active_mode_stat_1, and active_mode_stat_2 carry the
  standard summary totals.
- Temporary timer, target, spinner-value, helper-use, and inlane state remains
  in Python and is not stored as player variables.
