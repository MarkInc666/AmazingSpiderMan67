MASTER VINE — VINE INVASION

Core rules
- The rooftop gate starts open.
- Entering the upper playfield starts one of three waves, clears all lower shot
  states, resets both drop banks, closes the gate, and starts a 30-second timer.
- Each upper-spinner pulse scores 25K, lights one random unique available lower
  shot, and resets the wave timer to 30 seconds.
- The 22 lower objectives are both web targets, both slings, both pops, all
  three saucers, all eight drops, A, B, middle A, middle B, and STAR.
- A programmed objective pulses. Collecting it scores 50K and leaves its lamp
  solid at reduced brightness. Each objective scores once per wave.
- When waves 1 and 2 expire, the gate opens. Existing lower shot states remain
  available until the player returns upstairs; that return clears the states
  and begins the next wave.
- The third timer expiry ends the mode immediately.

Case Files
- More Jackpots: the first spinner pulse of each wave lights three random
  unique lower shots instead of one.
- Bigger Jackpots: lower-shot awards are 75K instead of 50K.
- More Time: all three wave timers are 40 seconds instead of 30 seconds.
- Safety Net: starts the standard 10-second Case File ball save on the first
  upper-playfield entry.
- Shot Assist: the first lower jackpot of the mode also collects another random
  lit, uncollected shot. If no second lit shot exists, the original shot scores
  a second time.

Hardware notes
- While the rooftop gate is open, a five-step chase runs in this physical order:
  spinner insert; left-lower GI and spinner GI together; left-upper GI; ramp
  GI; and the top GI beside the VUK. The chase clears as soon as the VUK
  captures the ball.
- During an active rooftop wave, the GI directly below the upper spinner
  flashes to identify the spinner shot. This flash begins on VUK capture.
- The three left drops share l_3bankbonus, so the display names the selected
  left target while the shared bank insert pulses whenever any left drop is lit.
- Drop targets already knocked down during a wave are excluded from later
  random programming until the next upper entry resets both banks.
- The mode disables Daily Bugle Mystery, explicitly ejects the VUK, and restores
  Daily Bugle state when it stops.
