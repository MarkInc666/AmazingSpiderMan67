DR. VON SCHLICK — OIL PELLETS / REACTOR FLOOD
================================================

STATUS
------
Implemented. Needs on-machine playtest.

CORE FLOW
---------
1. Follow the moving flashing green shot and collect five Oil Pellets.
2. The rooftop gate opens and the VUK lights for a 1,000,000 Super Jackpot.
3. The shared Master Vine route chase runs from the lower spinner through the
   ramp to the VUK until the shot is made or the timer expires.
4. Shoot the VUK before its 20-second timer expires.
5. The qualified VUK shot immediately cancels any pending shared VUK eject or
   retry and transfers the occupied VUK to the villain-summary hold.
6. Ball remains physically held while the four-second, eight-band reactor flood
   animation plays.
7. The Super Jackpot is awarded at the final flood band and the mode ends.
8. Ball remains in the VUK for the complete villain summary, then ejects when
   the summary finishes or is skipped.

The VUK must not eject between the qualified shot, flood animation and summary.
An unqualified VUK hit still ejects normally.

SCORING
-------
- Oil Pellets 1-5: 100,000 each.
- Bigger Jackpots: Oil Pellets 1-5 score 150,000 each.
- With More Jackpots, optional Pellet 6 scores 200,000 and Pellet 7 scores 250,000.
- With More Jackpots + Bigger Jackpots, Pellet 6 scores 300,000 and Pellet 7 scores 350,000.
- Reactor Flood Super Jackpot: 1,000,000.

CASE FILES
----------
More Jackpots
  Two additional Oil Pellets remain available after the Super is qualified.
  Pellet 6 is worth 200,000 and Pellet 7 is worth 250,000.

Bigger Jackpots
  The first five Oil Pellets score 150,000. With More Jackpots active too,
  Pellet 6 is worth 300,000 and Pellet 7 is worth 350,000.

More Time
  Super timer is 30 seconds instead of 20.

Safety Net
  Starts the Case File ball save when the Super is qualified.

Shot Assist
  The first collected Oil Pellet counts twice.

LIGHTING
--------
- Base GI is E0E040.
- The current moving shot flashes green at 250ms on / 250ms off.
- The reactor flood remains the eight-band blue rise.

PLAYTEST CHECKLIST
------------------
- Pending shared VUK ejects/retries are cancelled when the mode starts.
- A qualified VUK hit immediately arms the summary hold.
- The ball remains in the VUK throughout all eight flood bands.
- The ball remains held throughout the villain summary.
- Summary completion or skip ejects the VUK ball exactly once.
- An unqualified VUK hit still ejects normally.
- The route chase stops immediately when the qualified VUK switch closes and
  also clears on timeout or mode stop.
