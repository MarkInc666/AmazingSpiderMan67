DOCTOR ZAPP — CAMERA FLASHES
Status: Implemented / needs playtest
Chapter: Nature Strikes Back

CORE RULES
- Hit one target from the left drop bank and one target from the right drop bank.
- The first qualifying hit in each bank scores 100,000; Bigger Jackpots scores 150,000.
- When both banks are qualified, rooftop access opens and stays open for the rest of the mode.
- Bank progress, camera flashes, and upper-target upgrades remain if the ball leaves the roof.

ROOFTOP PHASE
- The upper spinner is always active after rooftop access opens.
- Each spinner spin scores 50,000, or 75,000 with Bigger Jackpots.
- Each spin adds one camera flash plus one additional flash for every unique upper target collected.
- The three unique upper targets each score 50,000, or 75,000 with Bigger Jackpots.
- Each unique upper target permanently adds +1 camera flash per spinner spin.
- Repeated upper-target hits have no extra mode award.
- Doctor Zapp is defeated immediately at 25 or more camera flashes.

CASE FILE HELPERS
- Safety Net: 10-second ball save at mode start.
- More Time: 15-second ball save on the first upper-playfield entry only.
- Bigger Jackpots: bank qualifications 150K; upper targets and spinner spins 75K.
- Shot Assist: the first unique upper target randomly awards another uncollected upper target, including its score and +1 flash-per-spin upgrade.
- More Jackpots: after 25 flashes, keep the upper flipper enabled for a 10-second victory spinner window. Spins score normal points only and do not add flashes. At expiry, start the upper-flipper lockout and summary.

DEFEAT / FLIPPER HANDOFF
- Without More Jackpots, reaching 25 flashes immediately requests a six-second upper-flipper lockout and completes the mode.
- With More Jackpots, the lockout begins after the 10-second bonus window.
- Only the right upper flipper is disabled; lower flippers remain active.
- The persistent base mode owns the six-second timer so it survives Doctor Zapp teardown and summary startup.
- No additional defeat ball save is awarded.

LIGHTING
- Start GI dark blue.
- Both bank GIs pulse blue; a qualified bank turns solid blue.
- One-bank progress uses a blue GI pulse.
- Both banks qualified changes GI to bright electric blue and opens rooftop access.
- All upper-target lamps pulse white; collected and Shot Assist-awarded targets turn solid white.
- First-time upper-target awards give a brief white GI flash.
- Reaching 25 flashes gives a full white flash.
- More Jackpots victory window pulses blue/white.
- Spinner hits retain the existing shared spinner GI flash; this mode adds no duplicate spinner flash.
