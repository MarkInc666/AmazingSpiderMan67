ASM67 VUK RELEASE HANDLING
==========================

The Daily Bugle VUK is a raw switch/coil pair, not an MPF ball device. A mode
that disables Daily Bugle Mystery must therefore own every VUK hit explicitly.

Shared handling
---------------

- Daily Bugle enabled, Mystery not ready: eject after 500 ms.
- Daily Bugle enabled, Mystery ready: award Mystery and eject after 5 seconds.
- Chapter mini-wizard start: villain progression holds the ball through the
  intro and releases it when the intro finishes or is skipped.
- A villain ending at the VUK posts villain_summary_hold_vuk_until_done.
  Villain Bookends keeps Daily Bugle disabled, releases the ball when the
  summary finishes or is skipped, then restores Daily Bugle after the release
  and retry window.
- request_vuk_eject with delay_ms schedules a release from the persistent
  villain-progression mode, so the release survives the requesting mode ending.
- Every up_kick pulse is checked after 900 ms. If s_vuk_switch is still active,
  up_kick_retry pulses the VUK coil one additional time.

Mode ownership rules
--------------------

- Modes that score or time the VUK must disable Daily Bugle Mystery on start,
  cancel its pending VUK eject, and restore Daily Bugle on stop.
- Neutral VUK hits normally use a 1,000-1,500 ms delayed release.
- Mode-ending VUK shots hold for the villain summary rather than using a fixed
  eject delay.
- Super Swami counts an unlit VUK as an upper-left restoration shot, then
  ejects. A VUK hit that qualifies the Blackout Jackpot cannot collect that
  Jackpot on the same switch event.
- Dr. Manta intentionally holds its first VUK ball until a second ball locks in
  a saucer. Its later releases remain mode controlled.
- Clive and Blotto and Plutonians leave Daily Bugle enabled intentionally; the
  VUK can count for their area rules while Daily Bugle owns the release.
