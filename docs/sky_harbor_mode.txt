BARON VON RANTENRAVEN — SKY HARBOR

Gameplay identity
- Limited-flip rooftop precision mode.
- Distinct from Vulture, whose upper-playfield identity is spinner volume and value building.

Flow
1. Hit any saucer to open roof access.
2. Enter the upper playfield to begin a Sky Harbor assault with 10 flips.
3. Hit all three unique upper targets before the flips run out.
4. The first two unique targets award jackpots.
5. The third unique target awards the Super Jackpot, boosted by remaining flips, and completes the mode.
6. Re-hitting a completed target scores 250K.
7. Running out of flips ends that roof attempt; the player can qualify another attempt after returning downstairs.

Case files
- More Time: 15 flips instead of 10.
- Bigger Jackpots: raises target jackpots, Super base, and remaining-flip bonus.
- More Jackpots: repeated completed targets score 500K instead of 250K.
- Safety Net: starts the case-file ball save at mode start.
- Shot Assist: the first unique target also spots another unhit target.

Skymaster
- The former limited-flip roof-target rules have been removed from Skymaster.
- Skymaster now uses a distinct non-rooftop ordered-drop mode: the upper
  spinner forces the eight lower targets down from left to right, correct
  direct hits can advance the same sequence, and wrong hits reset their bank.
