🏖️ Sandman
Mode idea: “Shifting Sands”

- Raise the right 5-bank and flash one moving target.
- The target advances every 3 seconds, or every 5 seconds with More Time.
- When the timer expires, the currently flashing target auto-drops and the light advances.
- The right-bank rubber restarts the current move timer.
- Non-flashing drops score 25K.
- Flashing drops score 200K, or 350K with Bigger Jackpots.
- Consecutive flashing hits award 500K / 1M / 2M / 5M for runs of 2 / 3 / 4 / 5.
- Clear 3 banks, or 4 with More Jackpots.
- Shot Assist scores the first timed auto-drop as a flashing hit.
- GI pulses yellow for 750 ms and white for 250 ms, restarted with each shift timer.
- Active target flashes green/white; other standing target inserts remain dim blue.
