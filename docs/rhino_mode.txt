# RHINO BASH

## Core loop

- Pops advance Rhino through five Rage levels.
- Every other configured Rhino Smash switch adds the current Rage amount to the pending jackpot.
- The always-flashing left B lane (`s_inlane_m_l`) collects the current jackpot.
- Each collected jackpot resets Rage to level 1 and starts the next jackpot at its new base value.
- Five jackpots complete the mode; More Jackpots adds a sixth cycle.

## Rage progression

| Rage | Pops required | Add per Smash |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 | 5K |
| 2 | 2 | 10K |
| 3 | 3 | 25K |
| 4 | 4 | 50K |
| 5 / Berserk | 5 | 100K |

The pop requirements are cumulative within each jackpot cycle. Pops score normally but do not also add the Rage amount to the jackpot.

## Jackpot bases

- Jackpot 1: 100K
- Jackpot 2: 150K
- Jackpot 3: 200K
- Jackpot 4: 250K
- Jackpot 5: 300K
- Jackpot 6 with More Jackpots: 350K

Bigger Jackpots adds 100K to every base value.

## Berserk

- Reaching Rage 5 starts a 10-second collect timer.
- More Time extends every Berserk timer to 15 seconds.
- Smash switches continue adding 100K during Berserk.
- Missing the collect ends the mode.
- Shot Assist automatically awards one missed Berserk jackpot, once per mode.

## Lighting

- GI steps from yellow through orange to red as Rage rises.
- Berserk uses rapidly flashing red GI.
- Left B and its associated collect insert flash throughout the mode.
- Rooftop gate remains under normal Daily Bugle control.

## Case Files

- More Jackpots: adds a sixth jackpot cycle using the same five-pop progression and Berserk timer.
- Bigger Jackpots: adds 100K to every jackpot base.
- More Time: adds 5 seconds to Berserk.
- Safety Net: standard 10-second ball save.
- Shot Assist: saves one missed Berserk by awarding the jackpot automatically.
