FINAL WIZARD END FLOW
=====================

1. Final Showdown multiball ends.
2. final_showdown_mode_complete is posted and the current player is marked retired.
3. At that moment, snapshot future balls:
   unused normal balls + queued earned extra balls.
4. Kingpin / Final Showdown summary runs.
5. After the summary, flippers/autofire are disabled and the remaining physical ball drains naturally.
6. Normal ball_ending Bonus runs.
7. Hold Bonus is resolved as an effective last-ball award.
8. BALLS REMAINING awards 10,000,000 per snapshotted future ball.
9. TOTAL BONUS is shown and Bonus finishes.
10. Queued extra balls for the retired player are consumed.
11. If another non-retired player has a normal ball remaining, MPF rotates and player_retirement skips retired players.
12. If nobody remains eligible, player_retirement calls game.end_game(), preserving MPF's normal game_will_end, game_ending, high-score, game_ended, and attract flow.
