DAILY BUGLE MYSTERY — PICTURE BALANCE

- A and B still qualify access and open the rooftop gate.
- Each qualifying rooftop-spinner hit adds one to the player's running picture total.
- The status display reads PICTURES TAKEN: X.
- Mystery costs progress by collection: 1 picture, then 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8,
  9, and 10. The tenth and every later Mystery continue to cost 10 pictures.
- If the current cost is already stored, completing A+B opens the gate and makes
  Mystery available immediately.
- Collecting Mystery spends the current cost and resets the current A+B cycle.
  Surplus pictures remain banked for the next qualification.
- The status display shows both PICTURES TAKEN and NEXT MYSTERY cost.
- The picture balance persists across balls, villain interruptions, and the normal
  Daily Bugle cycle reset posted after a villain summary.

Extra Ball milestones
---------------------
- Mystery 3 lights the left-bank Extra Ball. Completing the lit left bank
  consumes that lit award and queues one Extra Ball through the shared MPF
  Extra Ball group.
- Mystery 7 directly awards one Extra Ball through the same group, even when no
  banked Extra Ball is currently lit.
- Mystery 10 lights the right-bank Extra Ball. The right-bank light event also
  lights the shared MPF Extra Ball group; completing the lit right bank consumes
  that award and queues one Extra Ball.
- Left-bank, right-bank, and direct awards share the three-per-game limit.

Award presentation
------------------
- Every collected Daily Bugle Mystery starts the `db_award` widget immediately.
- The newspaper video begins with its award label blank.
- The selected award is chosen immediately but its gameplay event does not fire
  until 3 seconds after the VUK entry.
- At the same three-second point, `daily_bugle_award_text` is filled and the
  newspaper label updates. Dynamic Case File and Next Villain names settle on
  the event queue before the text is published.
- Random Case File awards use the selected Case File name. START NEXT VILLAIN
  awards use the selected villain name.
- The old generic award messages are removed. Mystery Case Files and Mystery
  Next Villains no longer post a second mode-message overlay, and the ordinary
  Extra Ball widgets are suppressed while the Daily Bugle award owns the VUK.
- Ordinary awards use a six-second newspaper presentation: 3 seconds blank,
  then 3 seconds with the award name before Daily Bugle ejects the VUK ball.
- START NEXT VILLAIN, EXTRA BALL, and COLLECT BONUS transfer VUK ownership to
  their award handlers when the award is revealed at 3 seconds. The newspaper
  remains visible for the second 3-second phase. At 6 seconds the handler takes
  over its presentation. Custom Bonus uses a compact 800 x 640 center widget
  with a blue background, BONUS above the centered comma-separated amount, and
  a Godot z-index of 4090, above the Daily Bugle
  widget's 4000 layer. The shared Villain Intro widget also uses z-index 4090 so
  START NEXT VILLAIN takes over above the newspaper layer. Widget-player YAML
  priority is intentionally omitted because MPF-GMC treats it as a mode-priority
  offset, not an absolute visual layer. Custom Bonus runs for 3 seconds; Extra
  Ball runs for 6 seconds. Each handler releases the ball through the shared
  checked VUK eject service when its presentation is complete.
- BONUS X is removed from the random award pool while the player's bonus
  multiplier is already at the 5X maximum.
- VILLAIN READY can be awarded once per villain qualification cycle. When
  awarded, it sets all three villain saucers to the maximum level. It becomes
  eligible again only after a villain mode has ended.

Audio routing
-------------
- `news_views_start` plays on the effects bus exactly when the Daily Bugle
  award newspaper widget starts. It is not tied directly to every VUK switch
  closure, so non-award VUK entries do not trigger it.
- The rooftop-spinner photo click, upper-target bells, and sling-swap callouts
  remain as designed.
- VUK impact does not play an electronic effect on switch closure. The shared
  `hb_spring_1` effect plays only when `up_kick` or its switch-confirmed retry
  actually fires the VUK coil.
- A+B completion is the sole voice-pool trigger. After A+B opens the gate, the
  already-updated picture count selects exactly one pool.
- Too few stored pictures selects the gate-open/more-photos pool. Its current
  line is `sm_i_better_take_some_photos`.
- Enough stored pictures selects the Mystery-ready pool; it does not also play
  the more-photos instruction.
- Reaching the required photo total later through rooftop-spinner progress posts
  `daily_bugle_photos_complete` as a separate future audio trigger. It currently
  updates the Mystery-ready state, widget and lighting without playing a voice.
- Completing A+B with an already-sufficient stored photo balance does not post
  `daily_bugle_photos_complete`, because no new photo threshold was crossed.
- The current Mystery-ready pool contains `sm_i_hope_mr_jameson_likes`,
  `sm_i_wonder_what_mr_jameson`, `sm_lets_get_to_the_daily_bugle`,
  `sm_these_photos_will_make_front_page`, `jj_wheres_that_parker_kid`, and
  `sm_i_better_collect_my_reward`.
