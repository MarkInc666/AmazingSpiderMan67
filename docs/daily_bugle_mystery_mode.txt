DAILY BUGLE MYSTERY — PICTURE BALANCE

- A and B still qualify access and open the rooftop gate.
- Each qualifying rooftop-spinner hit adds one to the player's running picture total.
- The status display reads PICTURES TAKEN: X.
- Mystery costs progress by collection: 1 picture, then 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8,
  9, and 10. The tenth and every later Mystery continue to cost 10 pictures.
- If the current cost is already stored, completing A+B opens the gate and makes
  Mystery available immediately.
- Collecting Mystery spends the current cost and resets the current A+B cycle.
  Surplus pictures remain banked for the next qualification.
- The status display shows both PICTURES TAKEN and NEXT MYSTERY cost.
- The picture balance persists across balls, villain interruptions, and the normal
  Daily Bugle cycle reset posted after a villain summary.

Audio routing
-------------
- The rooftop-spinner photo click, upper-target bells, and sling-swap callouts
  remain as designed.
- VUK impact does not play an electronic effect on switch closure. The shared
  `hb_spring_1` effect plays only when `up_kick` or its switch-confirmed retry
  actually fires the VUK coil.
- A+B completion is the sole voice-pool trigger. After A+B opens the gate, the
  already-updated picture count selects exactly one pool.
- Too few stored pictures selects the gate-open/more-photos pool. Its current
  line is `sm_i_better_take_some_photos`.
- Enough stored pictures selects the Mystery-ready pool; it does not also play
  the more-photos instruction.
- Reaching the required photo total later through rooftop-spinner progress posts
  `daily_bugle_photos_complete` as a separate future audio trigger. It currently
  updates the Mystery-ready state, widget and lighting without playing a voice.
- Completing A+B with an already-sufficient stored photo balance does not post
  `daily_bugle_photos_complete`, because no new photo threshold was crossed.
- The current Mystery-ready pool contains `sm_i_hope_mr_jameson_likes`,
  `sm_i_wonder_what_mr_jameson`, `sm_lets_get_to_the_daily_bugle`,
  `sm_these_photos_will_make_front_page`, `jj_wheres_that_parker_kid`, and
  `sm_i_better_collect_my_reward`.
