THE WEB TIGHTENS — CHAPTER 5 WIZARD
===================================
Status: APPROVED / CODED 2026-09-01 — NEEDS MACHINE PLAYTEST

CORE LOOP
---------
- Starts as 2-ball multiball.
- Player locks one ball in the Daily Bugle VUK.
- The VUK ball remains locked while five villain-inspired phases are played.
- A saucer starts each phase. The phase name is announced for 2 seconds before
  that phase gameplay begins.
- While a phase is waiting to be started, all three saucer hole lamps pulse white.
- Saucers park excess balls during a phase, but the mode always preserves at
  least one loose playable ball. If attrition leaves only held saucer balls and
  the VUK lock, one saucer ball is released automatically.
- Metal-Eating Monster and Harley & Clivendon add a ball at phase start.
- Mode ends when multiball falls to one ball in play.

SCORING / WIZARD PREP
---------------------
- Base 1X phase Jackpot: 100,000.
- Chapter Wizard Prep adds 20,000 per collected chapter Case File to every 1X
  phase Jackpot (0-25 Case Files = +0 to +500,000).
- A 2X Jackpot doubles both the 100K base and the Wizard Prep amount.
- Each successful phase adds 1,000,000 to that cycle's rooftop Super Jackpot.
- The rooftop Super Jackpot does NOT receive the Wizard Prep bonus.
- One Super Jackpot may be collected per cycle.

PHASE ORDER
-----------
1. FIDDLER
   - Saucer holds the initiating ball while a two-note sequence is shown.
   - Uses the same four note shots as Fiddler:
       Left Web / Left Bank / Right Pop / Right Bank.
   - Sequence may repeat a shot.
   - If there are other loose balls, the initiating saucer ball remains parked
     while the player reproduces the pattern.
   - If only the VUK ball + saucer ball remain, release the saucer ball after
     the WATCH presentation so the pattern can be played.
   - Additional balls entering saucers may also be parked as long as at least
     one loose playable ball remains.
   - Correct Note 1: 2X Jackpot.
   - Correct Note 2: 2X Jackpot and phase SUCCESS.
   - Wrong note: phase FAIL.

2. METAL-EATING MONSTER
   - Add-a-ball at phase start.
   - Uses the established six playfield zones from Fifth Dimension Curse.
   - First attack begins immediately; new attacks normally begin every 5 sec.
   - Saving a zone accelerates the next attack to 2 sec.
   - Each attacked zone has a 12-second destruction timer.
   - Each saved zone: 1X Jackpot.
   - Save 4 zones: phase SUCCESS.
   - Lose 3 zones: phase FAIL.

3. THE CONQUISTADOR
   - No timer on the setup steps.
   - Hit one physical left-bank drop: 1X Jackpot.
   - Make 3 LOWER spinner spins (s_web_spinner): 1X Jackpot per spin.
   - After spin 3, center web lights for 14 seconds.
   - Center web: 1X Jackpot and phase SUCCESS.
   - Center-web timer expiry: phase FAIL.

4. SPIDER-SLAYER
   - Uses Spider-Slayer's existing shot pool:
       Left Web, Center Web, Left Sling, Right Sling, Left Pop, Right Pop,
       Left Bank, Right Bank, Star, Saucers.
   - Starts with one random lit shot.
   - Each lit shot has its own 8-second lifetime.
   - Hitting a lit shot:
       * awards 1X Jackpot,
       * leaves that shot lit until its original timer expires,
       * adds one new random unlit shot while fewer than 7 required hits have
         been made.
   - Hit 7 lit shots: phase SUCCESS is locked in and no new shots are added.
   - Already-lit shots remain active and may still score until they expire.
   - After success, the phase ends when the final lit shot expires.
   - If all lit shots expire before 7 hits: phase FAIL.

5. HARLEY & CLIVENDON
   - Add-a-ball at phase start.
   - Uses the same established six playfield zones.
   - Complete 4 unique zones; each completed zone awards 1X Jackpot.
   - After the fourth unique zone, Star lights for 14 seconds.
   - A subsequent Star hit awards 2X Jackpot and phase SUCCESS.
   - The same Star hit that completes zone 4 cannot also collect the newly-lit
     Star Jackpot.
   - Star timer expiry: phase FAIL.

ROOFTOP SUPER / REPEAT CYCLE
----------------------------
- After all five phases have been played, successful or failed:
  - open the rooftop,
  - release the VUK ball to the roof,
  - keep any parked saucer balls held for 6 seconds after the VUK kick,
  - then release the first parked saucer ball, staggering any additional held
    saucer balls by 2 seconds,
  - light all three upper targets for one grouped Super Jackpot attempt.
- Super value = 1,000,000 x successful phases in that cycle (0-5M).
- The Super Jackpot is one grouped rooftop shot: left, center, or right upper
  target all collect the same Super Jackpot.
- All three upper targets flash together while the Super is available.
- When the VUK kicks the locked ball to the roof, display:
    HIT ANY UPPER TARGET
    FOR SUPER JACKPOT
- After the one Super is collected, the rooftop closes and the VUK is relit.
- If a ball is locked in the VUK before the Super is collected, that Super
  attempt is forfeited and a new five-phase cycle begins.
- Locking another ball in the VUK resets the cycle success count and begins at
  Fiddler again.

PHYSICAL / MACHINE TEST ITEMS
-----------------------------

  delayed-eject interference.

  from switch chatter.

- Verify a second/third saucer ball is held only when another loose ball remains.
- Verify add-a-ball launch timing in Metal and Harley phases.
- Verify six-zone mapping and lighting are readable during multi-ball play.
- Verify lower spinner (s_web_spinner) is the Conquistador spinner.
